## Paw Swat (shoot to clear)
- A hanging wall of coins in a honeycomb. The cat sits at the bottom with the next coin above its head. Drag to aim (a dotted line shows the path, including wall bounces), let go to swat. Tap the NEXT coin to swap it in. Letting go on the cat cancels the shot.
- 3 or more of one colour joined together clear, one after another, each tick a little higher in pitch. Anything left with nothing holding it falls and is counted as it drops. A big "+N" counts up ("Nice" from 7, "Great!" 12, "HUGE!" 18, "JACKPOT!" 25), with a coin-spill sound and sparkles from 8 and the cat's grin face from 10.
- Every few shots that clear nothing, the ceiling drops one row ("drop in N" in the top bar). Clear the wall to win; a coin on the red line loses. Each puzzle deals coins in a fixed order and only deals colours still on the wall.
- Puzzles (hand-built): 1 Warm-up (3 colours, striped wall); 2 Two hooks (everything hangs from two yellow hooks: the second hook drops the lot); 3 Purple knot (a middle heap hangs from one purple knot between two side chains); 4 Big cat (a coin cat face hanging by its ears).

## Cat Café (tap a cat, chain-collect)
- A tray of coins over a café counter. Three cats sit at the counter, each with a coloured bowl that shows how many coins it would take right now (×N, or – for none). Below: the cats still in line, in order.
- Tapping a cat pulls in every coin of its colour joined to the counter (up, down, left, right). The coins fly in one by one with a rising tick and a count-up, the cat's face changes (sweet, or the grin from 15), it leaves, the coins above drop, and the next cat in line sits down. A cat with nothing to take shakes its head and isn't used up.
- Clear the tray before the cats run out. Undo (top bar) goes back one cat; after a loss there is "Undo last cat".
- Puzzles: board shapes came from a search tool (`tools/cafe-solver.cjs`). I picked five and checked each with the solver (solvable; how forgiving the order is). Then the in-page bot solved all five with real taps.

## Stand-in art and sound
- Coins: tinted coins drawn in code, each colour with a white symbol (heart, drop, leaf, star, moon) so colours can be told apart on a small screen. The approved game coin art isn't used or changed.
- Cats: copies of the existing heads (Waiting, Sweet, Sharp Grin) and paw, scaled down only (`assets/`). Nothing redrawn.
- Sounds: trimmed copies of the existing coin sounds (accepted-coin tick, landing, coin spill), with pitch raised as the chain grows. Mute button top right; sound starts on the first tap.

## Testing
- Headless Chrome emulating an Android phone with touch (412x915 and 360x640 at 2x), driven only by touch events: `tools/capture.cjs` (`playtest`, `stills`, `swat-clip`, `cafe-clip`).
- Bot playtest at 360x640: Paw Swat won all 4 (puzzle 1: 12 shots; 2: 11 shots; 3: 23 shots with 1 ceiling drop; 4: 7 shots). Payoffs seen: puzzle 1 up to 16, 2 up to 27, 3 up to 8, 4: 41. Cat Café won all 5 by following a solver solution.
- No console errors. All sounds decode. Both layouts fit 360x640 without overlap.
- Fixed during testing: a finished puzzle's win/lose card could pop up over the next puzzle if you started one within a second (both pages).
- Not tested: a real phone (sound, touch feel, frame rate). Jenny's phone test is the real check.

## What felt good / weak (my own test)
- Paw Swat good: the falls on Two hooks and Big cat. The count-up while the coins fall gives the big-payoff feel.
- Paw Swat weak: puzzle 3 (Purple knot) is mostly small 3 to 6 clears; it plays more like a grind than a payoff. Big cat can be cut from the front (the bot did +41 through the blue top row without touching the ears), so it is easier than its name suggests. My bot aims perfectly, so human difficulty will be higher; the aim line helps.
- Cat Café good: the order puzzle works. On 3 to 5, serving the biggest bowl first loses, and the 20+ pulls come late, once earlier cats have merged the groups. The ×N on each bowl makes planning quick.
- Cat Café weak: the boards look blocky (big solid patches). The queue coins at the bottom are small.
