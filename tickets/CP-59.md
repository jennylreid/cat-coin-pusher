## Investigation (earlier the same day)
At this stage nothing in the game, `uat/` or `phone/` was changed or published.

## Summary
- **Cause:** a timing bug in how the Dance break pauses the round. Over the break the game holds the round timer and
  the bonus-drop clock still by saving them each frame and putting them back. If a break starts when the bonus clock
  has less than one frame left, the clock runs out every frame. Each time it does, the game drops a bonus and then
  puts the old value back. So a bonus prize, rain coin, Quake coin or music note falls **every frame** (60 a second
  in the harness).
- **Why it never ends:** the Wild 2 dance payoff waits for the sky to be empty before it finishes ("pour" stage). The
  flood of rain coins being pushed off keeps the sky raining, so the break never finishes. The break holds the timer
  (stuck at 0:06) and holds the win ("ALL 5! FINISH THE DANCE!"; all five prizes were in, as her screenshots show). It
  also keeps the bonus clock frozen. It is a closed loop; only leaving the board (Home) clears it.
- **Production 2026.11.16 is affected.** Its gameplay code is the same as UAT 70 (same five save-and-restore wrappers,
  same "wait for an empty sky"). It is rare: about 1 Dance break in 150 to 700 (details below). UAT 72 has the same
  code.
- **Proposed fix (simple, 3 lines):** the bonus clock doesn't run during a side game, and the dance payoff stops
  waiting after 4 s. The frozen pickup is untouched.
- **Jackpot proposal:** a rare Jackpot coin that starts an 8-second, capped, clean-ending cascade. Medium complexity.

## How often, and production
- The real game passes the actual frame time to the round (capped at 0.05 s); the harness uses a fixed 1/60 s.
- The loop starts if the bonus clock's remaining time, at the moment a Dance break starts, is shorter than a frame
  during that break. The clock counts down from 6-9 s.
  - At a steady 90 fps that is about 0.15% of breaks.
  - If any 50 ms frame happens during the break, it is about 0.7%. Long frames are common at a dance's start; her log
    shows 100-675 ms frames.
  - So it's roughly 1 Dance break in 150 to 700.
- Tuxedo is the starting cat. Breaks come from music-note coins, "Dance again" and a full Happy Cat meter, often
  several per round, so a regular player will hit it now and then. Two sightings (Nov 12, Nov 16) fit that.
- **Production 2026.11.16 / APK17** is the same game as UAT 70. PROD-06 only changed the labels, save keys and
  Developer-only items; I diffed `work/prod06/release.html` against UAT 70. It can happen on production on any level
  where Tuxedo's Dance break runs.
- **Other cats:** Treat toss, Laser chase, Pet the cat and Mouse pounce use the same save-and-restore code, so they can
  also get a bonus every frame. Unlike the dance payoff, they end on their own timers. So the likely result is a burst
  of bonuses for the length of the side game, not an endless freeze. This is from reading the code; I didn't run it.
- **The drain she saw** (coins 194 → 99, Quakes 6 → 0) **is not reproduced.** In the harness, coins and Quakes only
  go up during the loop, because rain pays in and taps go to the dance rather than dropping coins. Nothing in the loop
  takes coins or Quakes on its own. Her own taps on the Quake button are the likely cause of the Quakes going down. I
  can't explain the coins without a log of that session. Open point: to pin it down, ask her to Send log right after
  the next sighting.

## Proposed fix (simple)
The exact lines are in `tickets/CP-59-evidence/proposed-fix.diff`. I tested them on a scratch copy only.
1. **The bonus clock doesn't run while a side game is on** (dance, toss, chase, pet, pounce). That is what the five
   wrappers meant to do, so normal play doesn't change. The bonus-every-frame bug goes away for all five side games.
2. **The dance payoff's "pour" and "settle" stages end after 4 s at most,** even if coins are still falling. They
   finish landing as usual. This is a safety net: a break, and a win held for it, always ends.
- **Not touched:** the frozen pickup (look, sizes, timing, sounds, multi-drop), rain amounts, the wheel, the timer
  rules, and the CP-48/51 caps.
- **Checks for the fix ticket:** the repro above ends with a win; normal Dance breaks still pause the timer and pay
  the same; Dance again, the wheel and a win during a dance still work; the other four side games still pause the
  timer; no frame errors.

## Jackpot proposal (for Jenny to decide separately)
"**JACKPOT!**", a deliberate, short version of what she saw:
- **Trigger (rare):**
  - A gold Jackpot coin that comes as a bonus drop about 1 round in 15 (at most one per round, never on Level 1).
  - It also comes from the wheel's existing JACKPOT 50 slice.
  - She pushes it off to start the Jackpot.
- **Fixed duration with a clean end:**
  - It lasts 8 s. The round timer pauses (shown as "JACKPOT 0:08" counting down).
  - A bonus drops every 0.25 s: prizes, Rain 5/10/25 and Quake coins in the usual mix, so the pickups pile up loudly.
  - At 0:00 the drops stop and "JACKPOT OVER" shows. Coins already falling land, and the timer starts again.
  - It also ends at once on a win, Pause, Home or end of round, and it never runs at the same time as a side game.
- **Cap on pieces:**
  - At most 32 bonus pieces per Jackpot.
  - No new drop while the board has more than 220 pieces.
  - Rain past the CP-48 budget lands as gold 5s, which already happens.
  - That keeps it near the 160-250 pieces normal play reaches. It won't approach the 350+ that gave her 10 fps.
- **The frozen pickup is used as it is.** The busy, noisy multi-drop feel is exactly what this shows off, so nothing
  in the pickup changes.
- **Complexity: medium.**
  - New: a coin face drawn in code, a timed state with its own end, a callout and the caps.
  - Reused: the existing bonus-drop, sky-drop and rain code.
  - Plus a Developer "Start a Jackpot now" button.
  - About 80-100 lines. It needs the bug fix above first.
- **Simpler option (simple):** make the existing wheel slice JACKPOT 50 trigger this 8-second cascade instead of 50
  coins. No new coin, but it can only happen after a perfect dance.
