## Follow-up: coins over the ledge (Jenny: "there's no sound when coins fall over the ledge"). Prepared on UAT 74, NOT published
Waiting for the PM's go, after CP-61's UAT 75.
- The patch is `work/cp57-money/build-edge.py`. It applies on top of any build that already has CP-57, so at the go it runs on UAT 75: `python work/cp57-money/build-edge.py <UAT 75 release.html> <sha> <out>`.
- Prep build: `work/cp57-money/release-edge.html` (on UAT 74).

**Why plain coins over the edge were silent** (found while measuring):
- The single coin-off is `coin-off.mp3`, which is the whole 2.46 s sumaga123 recording. The game plays it at volume 0.17 and **stops it at 0.85 s**, but the coin's hit in that recording only starts at about 0.96 s.
- So what plays is the quiet lead-in: peak −39.8 dB in the file, about 33 dB under the hit it was meant to be. That's effectively silent, even when it isn't blocked (`coins/calibration.json`, `todayCoinOffAsPlayedDb` −72.6 against `intendedCoinOffHitDb` −39.4).
- On top of that, the 6-recording cap and coin-off's 0.18 s cooldown skipped most of them (CP-46). Each one also held a recording slot for 0.85 s of near-silence, which added to the cap that skips the coin landing.

**What the patch does** (real recordings only; nothing code-made):
- **Every plain coin over the front edge** plays a real "coin into the tray" take through Web Audio. There are 6 takes:
  - `tray1` is the real coin hit inside `coin-off.mp3`;
  - the others come from the universfield spinning coin and voicebosch bounces;
  - each rotates, never the same twice in a row, with pitch ±6%.
- **Groups:**
  - 3+ coins within 0.35 s add a coin-spill bed (CP-57's coin-spill slices);
  - 10+ within a second add a longer pour bed.
- **Inside a group** each coin's take is softer (0.45–0.8), varied, jittered by up to 40 ms, and at most one per 50 ms. So a spill clatters into the tray instead of machine-gunning identical hits.
- **Level: 8 dB under the coin landing**, for takes and beds alike. That's my pick: the game's own coin-off level was 18 dB under the landing, too faint to hear. It's easy to change.
- **No longer played for plain coins:** the HTMLAudio coin-off/spill and CP-46's code-made spills and cascades. This frees recording slots for the coin landing.
- **Unchanged:** prizes, gold and power coins over the edge, and every other sound. Plain rain coins are plain coins, so they get the tray takes too: their "coin-off" is now audible.

**Silent coin-offs in a busy 60 s** (`work/cp57-money/measure-edge.cjs` → `tickets/CP-57-evidence/edge-coins-60s.json`; coins held down, a coin rain at 20 and 40 s, a quake at 30 s, the same seed; audio cut-offs, cooldowns and the cap as on the phone):

| Plain coins over the edge | Coins | Real take / bed | coin-off.mp3 played (inaudible part) | Code-made group | Covered by a group bed | **Silent** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UAT 74 | 34 | – | 8 | 1 | – | **25** (and the 8 were inaudible) |
| New | 36 | 33 | 0 | 0 | 3 | **0** |

**Checks:** `work/cp57-money/checks-edge.cjs` passes **9/9** (`tickets/CP-57-evidence/checks-edge.json`):

- 10+ coins in a second → a pour bed.

- A prize still rings the register in the bell's slot.

**Limits:**
- Not heard on a phone.

**What changed** (the full recipe is in `work/cp57-money/build.py`)
- **Prizes and coin-rain coins over the ledge** ring a **real cash register** instead of the bell.
  - The register plays in the bell's own slot, with the bell's exact timing: one per piece, at least 0.1 s apart, the 2nd and 3rd quieter, a 4th or later in one burst skipped. So the busy multi-drop feel is unchanged.
  - 5 takes rotate, never the same take twice in a row.
  - It plays at the bell's loudness.
  - The rest of the frozen pickup (look, sizes, timing, thump) is untouched.
- **Gold 5 rain coin:** the register on the same schedule, instead of the code-made ka-ching.
- **Bell split** (Jenny): power coins (Quake, music note, fish, laser, heart, mouse) and the **Jingle bell prize** keep the bell. The bell's code is unchanged.
- **Coins never plink** (added after UAT 71: "it's coins falling. It should sound like coins falling"). Every code-made coin plink is now a real coin recording, each set rotating its takes and matched to the loudness of the sound it replaces (`work/cp57-money/coins/calibration.json`):
  - a coin, rain coin or gold coin bumping or first touching the pile → **8 short coin-on-coin hits** (voicebosch);
  - a coin dropping in from the sky and the coin-rain "drip" → **6 coin-onto-pile takes**: the A takes Jenny liked (the "purr") and the C takes;
  - a coin tower toppling → **3 coin-spill slices**;
  - the Dance break payoff's coin pour → **2 longer coin-spill stretches**.
  - **Prizes keep their plink.** For the first moment after the game starts, while the takes decode, the old sound can still play.
  - These are all recordings already in the project folder (Pixabay Content License). No new downloads.
- **Plain coins going over the edge**, including the ones that fall in a coin rain, keep coin-off (Jenny).
- **Dance break music: +10 dB** through its own bus with the UAT 43 soft limiter. That covers the count-in, kick, snare, clap, hats, bass, lead, crash and end chord. Hit and miss sounds are unchanged.

## What plays today when a piece goes over the front edge (UAT 71)
Confirmed two ways:
- in the code (`work/cp71/release.html`);
- in the real game: `work/cp57-money/probe-edge.cjs` makes each piece at the front edge with the game's own `addCoin`, hands it to the game's own `fallOff`, and logs every sound call. Results are in `tickets/CP-57-evidence/edge-sounds-today.json`.

| Piece over the edge | Sound at the edge today | Afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| **Prize** (cheese, shrimp, tuna, yarn, duck, mouse, feather, …) | the **ledge bell** (`ledgeBell` → code-made `sfx.pickup('bell')` at 0.5), plus a happy-meter notch on Dance boards | the landing thump in the bag (frozen) |
| **Jingle bell prize** (`bell`) | the same ledge bell as any prize | the thump |
| **Coin rain coins** (rain 5 / 10 / 25) | the **ledge bell**, then the "COIN RAIN" call | its coins fall from the sky |
| **Gold 5 coin** | a code-made **ka-ching** (`sfx.kaching`), plus the gold shimmer every other time (CP-46). No bell | – |
| **Power coins:** Quake | the coin-off recording **and the ledge bell** | `sfx.special` when it reaches its button |
| **Power coins:** music note, fish, laser, heart, mouse | the **ledge bell**, then each one's own start sound | their side game |
| **Cat coins and recipe coins** | no bell: their own fanfare / "bam" | – |
| **Plain coin** (including plain coins that fell in a coin rain) | `coin-off.mp3` for one coin; a spill or cascade for groups (CP-46). A coin-rain coin also gets a code-made "drip" 30% of the time | – |

**How the bell is timed today** (frozen pickup timing): one ring per piece that flies to the pickup. Each ring starts at least 0.1 s after the last; the 2nd and 3rd in a burst are quieter (0.7, 0.5), and a 4th or later in one burst is skipped. The bell sits at half volume (`CP51_BELL` 0.5, from UAT 68).
