## Dance break bug (Jenny, UAT 77): investigation (Nov 16)
**Found and fixed (CP-61's engineer is folding it into UAT 78):** since UAT 75, an on-beat hit banks nothing.
- A CP-61 comment swallowed `D.bank[kind]++;` on the hit line.
- So the big coin counter under the lane stays at 0 and the payoff pays 0 coins.
- The one-line fix is `work/cp57-money/build-dancefix.py`.

**Not found: any change to the Dance break's sound, its timing or its hit window between UAT 73 and 77.** Measured on the desktop:
- Every sound call during a Dance break, with its time against the beat clock (`dance-sounds.cjs` → `dance-sounds.json`). UAT 73, 75, 76 and 77 make the same calls at the same beats (kick, snare, clap, hats, bass, lead, count-in, crash, end chord, hit dings, pusher, CP-46 notes). Nothing that marked the beat is missing or replaced. The only difference is that the beat goes through the louder bus (CP-57).
- Kick timing against the beat clock (`dance-sync.cjs`): within 0.04 ms in UAT 73, 74, 75 and 77. Judging is 16/16 with a tap on every beat in all of them.
- The rendered audio's beat shape (`dance-contrast.html`). The on-beat vs off-beat loudness contrast is unchanged by the louder bus and its limiter: 25.3 vs 25.1 dB full range, 15.0 vs 14.4 dB in the phone range (above 300 Hz). So the limiter doesn't shift the beat onto the off-beat.
- Jenny's phone log (`uat/diagnostics`, saved 20:23):
  - It has one Dance break, on **UAT 74** (louder music already in): 16/16 hits, taps 33–97 ms before the beat clock, auto offset at its −50 ms floor, and smooth frames during the dance (worst 33 ms).
  - Her **UAT 77** Dance breaks aren't in it: that session had only run a minute.

**Why the checks didn't catch the bank bug:** CP-61's checks and mine compared the Dance timing and hit judging, which really are unchanged. No check asked whether a hit pays out.

**Check added:** `work/cp57-money/dance-sync.cjs` taps exactly on every beat and fails unless:
- all 16 are hits;
- the bank holds 16;
- the payoff adds 16 coins;
- every kick is within 1 ms of its beat.

On UAT 75 and 77 it catches the bug (bank 0).

**Next, to pin down the sound:** one Dance break on UAT 78 on her phone, then the log. The game records every tap's offset from the beat (`dance-taps`), so if the sound is really late or early against the visuals, her offsets will show a shift or drift. Until then I have no evidence for a second cause, and I haven't changed anything speculatively.

**Why plain coins over the edge were silent** (found while measuring):
- The single coin-off is `coin-off.mp3`, which is the whole 2.46 s sumaga123 recording. The game plays it at volume 0.17 and **stops it at 0.85 s**, but the coin's hit in that recording only starts at about 0.96 s.
- So what plays is the quiet lead-in: peak −39.8 dB in the file, about 33 dB under the hit it was meant to be. That's effectively silent, even when it isn't blocked (`coins/calibration.json`, `todayCoinOffAsPlayedDb` −72.6 against `intendedCoinOffHitDb` −39.4).
- On top of that, the 6-recording cap and coin-off's 0.18 s cooldown skipped most of them (CP-46). Each one also held a recording slot for 0.85 s of near-silence, which added to the cap that skips the coin landing.

**What the patch does** (real recordings only; nothing code-made):
- **Every plain coin over the front edge** plays a real "coin into the tray" take through Web Audio. There are 6 takes:
  - `tray1` is the real coin hit inside `coin-off.mp3`;
  - the others come from the universfield spinning coin and voicebosch bounces;
  - each rotates, never the same twice in a row, with pitch ±6%.
- **Groups:**
  - 3+ coins within 0.35 s add a coin-spill bed (CP-57's coin-spill slices);
  - 10+ within a second add a longer pour bed.
- **Inside a group** each coin's take is softer (0.45–0.8), varied, jittered by up to 40 ms, and at most one per 50 ms. So a spill clatters into the tray instead of machine-gunning identical hits.
- **Level: 8 dB under the coin landing**, for takes and beds alike. That's my pick: the game's own coin-off level was 18 dB under the landing, too faint to hear. It's easy to change.
- **No longer played for plain coins:** the HTMLAudio coin-off/spill and CP-46's code-made spills and cascades. This frees recording slots for the coin landing.
- **Unchanged:** prizes, gold and power coins over the edge, and every other sound. Plain rain coins are plain coins, so they get the tray takes too: their "coin-off" is now audible.

**Silent coin-offs in a busy 60 s** (`work/cp57-money/measure-edge.cjs` → `tickets/CP-57-evidence/edge-coins-60s.json`; coins held down, a coin rain at 20 and 40 s, a quake at 30 s, the same seed; audio cut-offs, cooldowns and the cap as on the phone):
