# CP-74 Hug, step 1: layout prototype (Claude engineer, Nov 18)

This is a prototype to watch, not a release. I didn't touch `uat\`, `phone\` or any release file. It's built on UAT 91
(`work\cp91\release.html`, pinned by hash), and the work is in `work\cp74-hug\` and `tickets\CP-74-evidence\`.

## What it does (about 7 s in all; the sides are blocked for exactly 6 s)
1. **0 s.** "HUG!", a meow, the paw whoosh and Pet the cat's "mrrp". The idle mitts lift away behind the head.
2. **0–0.35 s.** The Ragdoll's **shoulders** come forward at the head's lower corners, behind the head.
3. **0.12–0.45 s.** **Thick furry arms** grow down both sides of the board. They are fur-coloured, with the cream side
   toward the board and fur tufts on the outer edge.
   - Each arm's inner edge sits on the table edge, so the arms are the gutter walls.
   - The walls are today's two Hug colliders, same size and place. They go up at 0.45 s, when the arms reach the front,
     and come down at 6.45 s, when the arms start to let go. What blocks the sides is what you see.
4. **0.35–0.6 s.** The forearms curl in under the front lip. The **approved mitts** slide in from the bottom corners and
   meet below the board.
   - The mitts are the same images at the same size (182) as the idle mitts, drawn the same way. They are only moved and
     turned in 0.3 rad, so the claws point toward each other.
   - At the clasp there's a burst of 36 hearts and Pet the cat's "content" trill.
5. **The hug (6 s).**
   - **Purring:** the Pet the cat purr engine (`sfx.purr`), steady the whole time.
   - **Hearts:** about 16 a second rise from the arms and mitts, and 6 a second pop up anywhere over the board.
   - **The mitts knead,** gently and in turn.
6. **Let go (6.45–7.05 s).** The mitts slide out, the arms pull back up, the shoulders sink back and the idle mitts drop
   back into place. The purr fades out.

**Nothing covers the play area.** The shoulders and arms are clipped to outside the table outline. The mitts sit below
the front lip (the visible palm starts at y 778, and the lip is at 771). They're drawn under the coins, so coins and
prizes going over the front edge, and the frozen pickup, stay in front of them. Only the hearts and today's
"NO SIDE FALLS" words cross the board.

## Checks (`work\cp74-hug\check.cjs`, `CP-74-evidence\check.json`, all pass)
- **The walls.** Both walls go up at 0.47–0.52 s and come down at 6.42–6.47 s (6 s blocked). None are left afterwards.
- **The lock.** A second tap mid-power does nothing and uses no charge.
- **Leaving mid-power.** Home or the round ending mid-power leaves no power and no walls behind. The purr stops through
