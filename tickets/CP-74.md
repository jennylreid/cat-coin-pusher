# CP-74 Hug, step 1: layout prototype (Claude engineer, Nov 18)

This is a prototype to watch, not a release. I didn't touch `uat\`, `phone\` or any release file. It's built on UAT 91
(`work\cp91\release.html`, pinned by hash), and the work is in `work\cp74-hug\` and `tickets\CP-74-evidence\`.

## What it does (about 7 s in all; the sides are blocked for exactly 6 s)
1. **0 s.** "HUG!", a meow, the paw whoosh and Pet the cat's "mrrp". The idle mitts lift away behind the head.
2. **0–0.35 s.** The Ragdoll's **shoulders** come forward at the head's lower corners, behind the head.
3. **0.12–0.45 s.** **Thick furry arms** grow down both sides of the board. They are fur-coloured, with the cream side
   toward the board and fur tufts on the outer edge.
   - Each arm's inner edge sits on the table edge, so the arms are the gutter walls.
   - The walls are today's two Hug colliders, same size and place. They go up at 0.45 s, when the arms reach the front,
     and come down at 6.45 s, when the arms start to let go. What blocks the sides is what you see.
4. **0.35–0.6 s.** The forearms curl in under the front lip. The **approved mitts** slide in from the bottom corners and
   meet below the board.
   - The mitts are the same images at the same size (182) as the idle mitts, drawn the same way. They are only moved and
     turned in 0.3 rad, so the claws point toward each other.
   - At the clasp there's a burst of 36 hearts and Pet the cat's "content" trill.
5. **The hug (6 s).**
   - **Purring:** the Pet the cat purr engine (`sfx.purr`), steady the whole time.
   - **Hearts:** about 16 a second rise from the arms and mitts, and 6 a second pop up anywhere over the board.
   - **The mitts knead,** gently and in turn.
6. **Let go (6.45–7.05 s).** The mitts slide out, the arms pull back up, the shoulders sink back and the idle mitts drop
   back into place. The purr fades out.

**Nothing covers the play area.** The shoulders and arms are clipped to outside the table outline. The mitts sit below
the front lip (the visible palm starts at y 778, and the lip is at 771). They're drawn under the coins, so coins and
prizes going over the front edge, and the frozen pickup, stay in front of them. Only the hearts and today's
"NO SIDE FALLS" words cross the board.

## Checks (`work\cp74-hug\check.cjs`, `CP-74-evidence\check.json`, all pass)
- **The walls.** Both walls go up at 0.47–0.52 s and come down at 6.42–6.47 s (6 s blocked). None are left afterwards.
- **The lock.** A second tap mid-power does nothing and uses no charge.
- **Leaving mid-power.** Home or the round ending mid-power leaves no power and no walls behind. The purr stops through
  the game's existing `wildExit` / `pgpEnd` paths.
- **No frame errors** in any run, at 412x915 or 360x640.
- **Speed.** In real Chrome the power plays in real time: 6.46 s of power time in 6.5 s. My first version drew the fur
  circle by circle and halved the frame rate. The arms are now stroked tubes, cached while they're fully in.
- **Purr.** It's on at gain 0.44 for the whole hug and off afterwards (traced in the recording). It's a low rumble, so
  it's quiet under the music in the video.

## Hearts and purring: what exists, what's missing
- **Exists, and used:**
  - Pet the cat's continuous purr (`sfx.purr`), its "mrrp" and "content" sounds;
  - the game's meow and paw whoosh;
  - the game's heart shape (`pgHeart`).
- **Missing:**
  - a real purr recording, if the code purr is too faint on the phone (sounds are approved only in-game);
  - painted hearts, which are optional: the code hearts read fine.

## Effort
- **Code: small (about half a day to ship).** It's 116 lines (`cp74-hug.js`): the layout, the clip, the cached arm
  drawing, the mitt move, the walls' timing, hearts and purr. It replaces today's Hug drawing in the same slot.
  - Release checks to add:
    - side falls with and without the power;
    - pause, Home and round end;
    - a Quake or sweep mid-hug (those keep the idle mitts' own motion, and the bottom mitts hide meanwhile);
    - 30 fps;
    - the frozen pickup unchanged.
- **Art for Codex (step 2): 3 pieces, one at a time, each on a fixed canvas from the numbers above.**
  - **Shoulders:** one painting, mirrored. Canvas 200 × 160 game units (about 800 × 640 px), centred on the shoulder
    centre and painted to sit behind the head.
  - **Left arm:** canvas from the shoulder to the mitt cuff, about 140 × 520 game units (560 × 2080 px). Upper arm,
    side run, and the forearm curling in under the lip. The inner edge stays on the template's table-edge line.
  - **Right arm:** the left arm mirrored. A separate painting only if Jenny wants them to differ.
  - The mitts need no art: they're the approved ones.
  - A risk for art: the arm's shape follows the board's perspective, so the paintings must use the template frame
    (`hug-numbers-412x915.png`). Other screen sizes reuse the same picture at the same anchor points.

## Choices (my picks; change any line)
```
1. Timing: arms in 0.6 s; sides blocked 6 s (0.45-6.45 s); let go 0.6 s; about 7 s in all
2. Arms: 49-60 thick, inner edge on the table edge, fur colour with a cream side toward the board
3. Mitts: approved size 182, meet below the board, claws turned in 0.3 rad, gentle kneading
```

## Limits
- Desktop and headless Chrome only. There's no phone, FPS or device-sound evidence.
- **Not measured:** side falls. The walls are today's colliders, unchanged, and only their timing moved.
- **The bottom mitts cover the "RAGDOLL / Collect all 5!" corner for 6 s.** The HUD text still draws on top.
- **Rest pose (sweet face) during the Hug:** the shoulders then draw after the head. I didn't see it happen in testing.
