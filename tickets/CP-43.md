# CP-43 Pet the cat (Cozy mode): UAT 47 (November 12, 2026)

**Board card text:** CP-43 Pet the cat: a new COZY mode in the MODES picker, built on Wild 2. Push off the heart coin,
then stroke the head and tap the ears; the purr builds. Stop at the peak purr for the most coins. Too much, and the
cat hisses. UAT 47.

**Published 2026.11.12 UAT 47 / APK7**, numeric **20261112200000**, game-only, UAT only. Built on the published UAT 46
(pinned by hash). Production (`phone/`) is unchanged; its hash is the same before and after.

## What to try (phone)
1. **MODES ▾ → COZY** (pink, heart icon). The goal card says COZY. It lists the heart coin, "Stroke the head, tap the
   ears" and "Stop at the peak purr". Tap TAP TO START.
2. **The heart coin.** A pink PET coin with a white heart falls as the 1st or 2nd bonus drop. Push it off the front.
   "PET THE CAT!" shows, and the round clock stops (a pause mark beside it).
3. **Stroke the head.** Drag a finger back and forth across the forehead. You should hear and feel:
   - a soft brush sound with each stroke;
   - the purr getting louder and faster;
   - the strip under the cat (not over the face) filling;
   - the head leaning into the stroke;
   - the eyes closing (the approved Happy eyes).
4. **Tap an ear.** The ear flicks, the whiskers twitch, and you hear a "mrrp".
   - Tapping anywhere else: the cat glances, and you see "pet my head!".
   - No coin drops while you're petting.
5. **The peak.** When the meter reaches the star, you hear a chime and see "PEAK PURR!".
   - Lift your finger (stay off for about ¾ s) or tap DONE.
   - A stop within about half a second of the peak is **PURR-FECT!**: 24 coins pour, a rain-10 coin falls, and the
     cat does the Dancing pose.
6. **Keep going past the peak.**
   - The ears flatten and you hear a growl ("A BIT MUCH…").
   - Then a **hiss**: the Sharp Grin face for a moment, and "HSSSS!". Only 2 coins.
7. **Stop early.** You get fewer coins ("MORE PETS?" or "SO CLOSE!").
8. **A win waits.** If the last prize drops while you're petting, the win card waits until petting ends.
9. **Other modes are unchanged:** Wild 2, Treat toss and Laser chase.
10. **Settings → Developer:**
    - "Start pet the cat now";
    - dials for Pet length (9 s), Peak timing (3.5 s of stroking) and Grumpy after (1.2 s past the peak);
    - the last result.

    The dials are saved and are in the performance log.

## Changes
- `work/cp47/pet47.js`: the Cozy mode (`registerWildMode` id `cozy`, `features.pet`). It adds:
  - the heart coin (drawn in code);
  - Pet the cat (strokes, ear taps, purr meter, peak, grumpy, hiss, scoring, coin pour);
  - head motion (ear flicks, whisker twitch, lean, shiver, hiss jolt).
    These are clip-and-rotate moves on the approved head art. The closed eyes are the approved Happy features over
    Sweet. No new art.
  - It also pauses the timer, holds the win, takes all touches while petting, adds the Developer dials and keeps a
    petting log (last 3 sessions).
- `work/cp47/pet-sounds.js`:
  - `sfx.pet(...)`: start, brush, mrrp, huh, peak, grr, hiss, content;
  - `sfx.purr(level,rate,growl)`: a looped purr engine.

  Both are scaled by the sound volume and silent when sound is off.
- `work/cp47/build.py` patches the pinned UAT 46 with one-line hooks: head transform and draw, closed eyes, pointer
  move/up, COZY on the win/lose card, and the performance log.
- How the modes were reconciled: Cozy uses CP-41's MODES picker, the same as Laser in UAT 46. There is no separate
  button.

## Limits and later
- **Later art ticket (suggested):** the hiss borrows the Sharp Grin (quake face). A proper grumpy or hiss face, and
  maybe a content "eyes-closed Sweet" painting, would be new art for a later ticket. I didn't draw any.
- Peak timing is randomised ±12% per session so it can't be memorised. The "perfect" window is 0.45 s past the peak.
- The 👆 hint is an emoji, so it may look different on Android.
- Phone feel (stroke speed on a real screen, purr loudness, FPS) needs Jenny's test. Desktop tests don't prove it.
