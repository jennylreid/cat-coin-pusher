# CP-73 rework: Formal inspection's words big and readable, action after them; power names fixed (UAT 92, Claude engineer, Nov 18)

**Published to UAT only: 2026.11.18 UAT 92 / APK7, numeric 20261118000003** (game-only, on UAT 91). Production untouched.

## Phone test (UAT 92)
Update the UAT app (game-only, no new APK). The build should read **2026.11.18 UAT 92**.
Shortcut: Settings → Developer → open **Engineer tools** → **Give powers (3 each)**, then play the Tuxedo's board.
1. **Big words first.** Tap the Tuxedo's power. The big "FORMAL / INSPECTION!" pops up in the middle of the board, as it
   did before UAT 90, alone, for about a second. The hat and bow tie go on at the tap. Nothing else happens while the
   words show: no monocle, no beam, no gold rings, no cane.
2. **Action after the words.** Once the words have gone, the monocle drops on, the beam visits the pieces, they light up,
   and the cane sweeps them over the front edge, exactly as before. No "HMM." at any point.
3. **SPLENDID. after the sweep.** When the sweep has finished, the big "SPLENDID." pops up in the middle, readable, and
   never on top of the cane or the lit pieces. (The swept pieces' pickup flies up past it, drawn in front of the words.)
4. **Power names (cat screen).** Open Cats. The Tuxedo now reads "Dance break · power: Formal inspection" with
   "A monocle spots the best pieces; the cane sweeps them forward." under it, and its count reads "Formal inspection ×n".
   Every cat has a one-line description of its power that matches what it does.
5. **Unchanged:** the pieces it takes; Zoomies (Orange cat), the Yawn (no coin eating) and the Jackpot; the pickup when
   many prizes drop at once. A callout such as "COIN RAIN ×5!" that comes up during Formal inspection now waits and
   shows right after it.

## What changed (on UAT 91)
- Jenny, Nov 18: "CP-73 rework: big 'FORMAL INSPECTION!' first, action starts only after it's gone; drop 'HMM.'; big
  'SPLENDID.' after the sweep. UAT 92." ("Do it.")
- "FORMAL INSPECTION!" is the big centred word again, drawn exactly like the old callout (same place, size, pop, tilt,
  outline and fade), shown for 1.2 s right at the tap. It no longer queues behind other callouts.
- Formal inspection's own timeline (monocle, beam, rings, cane, sweep, monocle off) is unchanged but starts 1.25 s after
  the tap, once the words have gone. The monocle's clink moves with it.
- "HMM." is gone.
- "SPLENDID." shows big 0.15 s after the sweep ends (the cane is at the front edge by then) for 1.1 s, only if anything
  was picked, as before.
- The power (and its locked button) now lasts 6.05 s instead of 4.4 s (+1.25 s lead, +0.4 s for SPLENDID.).
- Other callouts wait while Formal inspection plays and show after it. Without this, a "COIN RAIN ×5!" that queued during
  the words popped up over the beam (seen in the first stills).
- The pieces are still picked at the tap, by the same rules. The two random draws each word made (and "HMM."'s two) are
  kept at the same points of the power, so the game's random draws are as before.
- Files: work/cp92/build.py (7 edits on published UAT 91 + the block cp73-rework.js). UAT 90's small band words
  (cp73Say) are left in the code but no longer called.
