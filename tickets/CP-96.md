## What it does
- 8x8 grid of the game's gold paw coins, 10 sleeping cats hidden under them. The big tuxedo cat sleeps above the board (closed eyes, floating z's).
- **Tap a coin** to pick it up. It flips over to show a coloured number chip (1 blue, 2 green, 3 red, 4 purple, 5 orange, 6 teal, 7 slate, 8 dark): how many sleeping cats touch it.
- **Empty spot = cascade.** The whole connected area flips in a ripple outward from your finger (45 ms per ring), each coin pops with gold sparkles, one coin clink per ring climbing in pitch, and a "+N" counts up. 14 or more coins: a coin-spill sound and the cat smiles in its sleep (Sweet face) for a moment.
- **First tap is always safe** and always opens an area (no cat in the 3x3 around it).
- **Fair boards:** every board can be solved by logic from the first tap; no 50/50 guesses. Checked on 1,344 boards (21 days x every first tap): all fair, about 0.1 ms each.
- **Paw marks:** hold a coin (0.38 s, short buzz) or switch the bottom toggle to "Paw" and tap. The pill top left counts cats left. Tapping an opened number whose cats are all pawed opens its other neighbours (the classic shortcut).
- **Win:** all 54 coins picked up. The cat wakes with the Sharp Grin and dances, gold coins rain down with the spill sound, "All coins, no cats woken." with your time. Buttons: Practice board (random) or Replay daily.
- **Lose:** tap a cat and it yawns (the CP-58 yawn face on the tile and on the big cat, which stretches). The other cats are shown, still asleep; wrong paws fade. "You woke a cat. She stretched and went back to sleep." **Try again** is one tap: same daily board, fresh start.
- **Daily:** the board comes from the date ("Daily #1" is Nov 19). The cat spots also depend on where you tap first, so two players' boards differ. Best time per day is saved on the phone.
- Mute button top right; sound starts on the first tap.
