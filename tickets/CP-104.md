## Rules as built

- Tap a cat to send it straight along its arrow. If another cat blocks the path to the edge, it bumps and stays put. Clear paths have green arrows.
- A cat trots to the first free seat. The conveyor pauses at the next plate until a seated cat matches its bib color and symbol. If several match, the longest-seated match eats first. After three plates, the cat bounces and leaves, freeing its seat.
- Empty the room and all five seats to win. Fill all five seats with no match for the next plate and the counter jams. **See plate order** reveals the remaining queue.
- Five fixed puzzles range from seven cats with grouped plates to fifteen cats with mixed plates. Today’s board rotates through the five layouts by local calendar day. Restart preserves the selected puzzle and plate order.
- The five cat portraits and treat sprites use CatCoin artwork. The room, bibs, counter, plates and conveyor are drawn in code.

## Validation

Desktop Chrome touch emulation at both sizes played a winning route and a jam route on each of the five boards. The checks also covered blocked moves, restart, daily selection, queue preview, art decoding and viewport fit. The stills show First Seating. Phone play and difficulty feel are pending Jenny’s review. This is an offline prototype with no hosted release.
