5. **Pet the cat's purr meter fills sooner: 2.5 s of stroking to the peak instead of 3.5 s** (about 30% sooner). The peak still

## UAT 129: Level/Goal boards not reachable (Jenny, Nov 20). Fixed in UAT 130 (above)
**Cause (my CP-100 bug, since UAT 127): the touch that opens a list also taps a row in it.** Home's buttons act on `pointerdown`, so
Level boards and Goal boards open their list while the finger is still down. When the finger lifts, the phone's browser sends a
click at that spot, and the list is now under it:
- Level boards: the click hits a board row. The endless board starts on that board (Board 9 at 412x915 and 384x854, Board 7 at
  360x640).
- Goal boards: the click hits a goal board row. That goal board starts (Gate, Cup shot or Bumper, depending on the phone size).
- So the list never stays open, and it looks like a board started instead. Daily puzzles is not affected: CP-87 already ignores
  that click for 500 ms after its menu opens. I didn't give the CP-100 lists the same guard.
- My UAT 127 checks used mouse taps, where no click follows on the new list, so they passed.
**Not the cause** (real Chrome, real taps, at 412x915, 360x640 and 384x854 with long-lived saves):
- Levels passed, Developer on, Board lab played and won, a CP-92 wallet of 420, Catnip.
- Just after leaving the endless board by its Home button.
- Just after leaving a goal board.
- After a reload.
In every case both buttons draw (`endHomeFree`, `endBtn`, `cp100GoalBtn`, `PGA.L.play` all present), nothing covers them
(`elementFromPoint` is the canvas), and a mouse tap opens each list (10 rows and 12 rows).
- One separate case: with **Home layout B** (Developer > tuning, "B board view"), Home's main screen never shows, so Level boards,
  Goal boards and the Daily button aren't there, and PLAY starts the timed Level 1. Board lab's button was missing in B before
  CP-100 too. Not this bug; worth knowing if Jenny's phone is on B.
**Fix (`work/cp130/cp100-ghost.js`, appended to UAT 129; build name only otherwise; `build.py` checks the base SHA):** when a list
opens during a touch, the next click inside that list within 400 ms of the lift is ignored. Taps after that work normally.
Lists opened without a finger down (the Goal boards list coming back after a round) aren't affected. No change to Home, the lists,
Daily puzzles or play.
- Real touch on UAT 129: both lists close at once and a board starts (`tickets/CP-100-evidence/uat129-lists/uat129-touch-*`).
- Real touch on cp130 at all three sizes:
  - Level boards and Goal boards open and **stay open** (the ignored click is counted).
  - A second touch on Board 1 starts the endless board on Board 1.
  - A second touch on Cup shot starts Cup shot.
  - Daily is unchanged.
  - Stills: `cp130-touch-*-list-stays.png`.
- Mouse taps on cp130, all five save cases and three sizes: both lists open as before (`cp130-mouse-probe.json`).
- Probe: `work/cp130/probe129.cjs` (env `SC`, `SIZES`, `INDEX`).
- Not run: the harness suites. The change touches only the list's click handling, so the touch probe is the real test.
- **Build state:** `release-version.json` currently says "2026.11.20 UAT 130" / 20261119000041. That's provisional until the PM
  gives the number (`python setup.py cp129 <n>` rewrites it). Nothing is staged or published; there are no stage or publish

## Investigation: checks75 frames differ from UAT 94 on 127+ (PM, Nov 20). No game change, nothing re-baselined
**Verdict: harmless. The test's start runs one frame early. CP-100 changes nothing in timed play or drawing.** The Ragdoll's one leftover
difference is CP-101's Dance meter (UAT 128), which is expected.
- **Cause:** checks75's replacement start (CP-96 upkeep: `PGU.screen='board';PGU.fade=.25;pgStartRound()` when `WILD.phase==='card'`)
  runs on the **first** card frame. The reference's PLAY tap can't happen until a frame later, because `PGA.L.play` only exists
  after the card has been drawn once (the first pass finds it null and ticks). So the new build's round starts 1/60 s earlier.
  At the "before" frame that puts the pusher at stroke 0.401 instead of 0.4062 (pusherY 239.94 vs 240.75), and that is the
  plate-and-claw offset. The same shift moves the floating "Lost the shrimp!" text's timing by a frame relative to the after-yawn
  capture. The pieces, coins and yawn play the same, which is why check 5 passed.
- **Proof** (light runs, one at a time; `work/cp100-play/inv75/`, copies in `tickets/CP-100-evidence/inv75/`):
  - `inv75.cjs`, Tuxedo 360x640, state at each step:
    - UAT 94 tapping PLAY and UAT 126 tapping PLAY: start at tick 191.
    - UAT 126 and UAT 127 with the replacement start: start at tick 190.
    - **UAT 126 with the replacement start differs from UAT 126 tapping PLAY.** The start path makes the difference before CP-100 exists.
  - `checks75-tickfix.cjs` is checks75-on129 with one change: the replacement start runs only once PLAY exists, on the frame the tap
    would. Results on UAT 129:
    - Checks 3 and 5 pass.
    - Check 4: Tuxedo, Orange, Siamese and Tortie before and after are **pixel-identical to UAT 94**; only the Ragdoll differs.
    - Log: `tickfix.log`.
  - `bisect75-inv.cjs`, the bisect with the same one-line fix:
    - **UAT 127: Tuxedo and Ragdoll before and after pixel-identical to UAT 94 (0 px).**
    - UAT 128: the Ragdoll differs in 6,080 px, all in the top-right box under the gear. That is the Ragdoll's new **DANCE METER** from
      CP-101's waltz (`ragdoll-128-topright-new-vs-uat94.png`), the same thing checks75 already blanks for the Orange (UAT 120).
- **Test fix, if the PM agrees (CP-96's `gen-checks.py`; no baseline change needed):**
  1. In checks75 (and checks93, which uses the same start), only start the round once `PGA.L.play` exists:
     `if(pl)g.run("PGU.screen='board';PGU.fade=.25;pgStartRound()")`.
  2. Extend the Orange's Dance meter blanking (`FR`) to the Ragdoll for builds with CP-101.
  With both changes checks75 should pass 7/7 against unchanged UAT 94. I haven't edited CP-96's files.

**Short answer:** yes, for a new player, unless we move three things off the level path first. On a fresh save almost all
progression starts with passing levels: a pass unlocks a recipe, the recipe opens the Kitchen, cooking brings visitors to the
Porch, a photo makes that cat's coin drop, and the coin opens the cat. Round ends also fill the coin jar (the Shop's money) and
give Catnip. The endless board never ends a round or passes a level, so with PLAY going there and nothing else changed, a new
player stays on the Tuxedo with no recipes, no Kitchen and an empty jar. Players who have already passed levels keep what they
unlocked. Everything below is from reading the UAT 125 build; nothing was changed.
