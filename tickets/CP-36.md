## What was built (Wild board only)
- **Happy meter:** at the top right, next to the power buttons: a heart, "HAPPY CAT" and **4 notches**.
  - Every prize that goes over the front fills one notch, repeats included. A notch pops with a chime.
  - When the meter is full it pulses, and a **Dance break** starts once the prize's Pop pickup has been held up (within 1.3 s).
- **Music-note coin:** a code-drawn deep-blue coin with a gold double note and "DANCE". It has the same size and physics as a rain coin.
  - It arrives as a bonus drop (marker, whistle, thud, "MUSIC NOTE!"). One of the 2nd to 5th bonus drops of each round is a note. After that there's a 6% chance per drop of a second one.
  - A drop that replaces a lost prize always comes first.
  - Pushing the note off the front gives it the Pop pickup, and the meter fills at once.
- **Dance break (about 9 s):**
  - **Start:** "DANCE BREAK!" pops up over a beat lane under the cat's paws, with a 3-beat count-in (3, 2, 1). The cat plays the Dancing pose intro.
  - **The dance:** 16 beats at 109 BPM (8.8 s). The cat loops **one move**, "paw drum" (move 1 of the approved Dancing pose), one cycle per bar, in time with the music. The other moves are kept for later, and the Dance move dial switches between them.
  - **Music:** generated in code. A kick on every beat, snare and clap on 2 and 4, off-beat hats, a C–Am–F–G bass and a small lead that builds over the 4 bars.
  - **Beat indicator:** music notes slide in from both sides and meet a ring in the middle on each beat. The ring pulses on the beat and flashes gold when a beat is banked. Four dots show the beat in the bar, and the lane reads "TAP ON THE BEAT".
  - **Timers:** a pink bar along the lane and "N s" show the dance time left. The happy meter drains as the dance runs. The round timer **pauses** for the whole break and shows a pause mark.
  - **Taps:**
    - Any tap during the break is a dance tap: no coin drops. EXIT and Settings still work.
    - **On the beat**, a tap banks a gold coin, which flies into the **bank** under the lane ("× N", with a streak count). Every 8 in a row shows "GROOVY!".
    - Now and then (12%) an on-beat tap banks a **rain coin** instead (rain 5, or 1 in 4 a rain 10), shown in the bank.
  - **Off the beat**, a tap banks nothing and **costs the next beat** ("off beat", a dull thunk). That keeps fast tapping from paying: 15 taps a second banked 0 beats in the test.
  - **When the music stops:**
    - A crash and a big chord, then the cat **roars**: a bigger, louder growl, plus the cat's lunge.
    - The **screen shakes** for 0.9 s, and "COIN RAIN ×N!" (or "DANCE RAIN ×N!" with rain coins) comes with confetti and a pour of coin clinks.
    - Everything banked **pours in at once**, spread over about 1 s (up to 33 coins a second): rain coins first, landing on the board to be pushed off, then the gold.
    - If nothing was banked: "MISSED THE BEAT!".
  - **After the break:** the meter is empty and the round timer runs again.
- **Developer dials** (Settings → Developer mode → Dance break, under Wild board). They are saved on the phone and go in the performance log as `danceTuning` (the Wild timer and coins are now in it too, as `wildTuning`).
  - **Start dance break now**: opens the Wild board if needed and starts a break right after GO, or straight away in a running round.
  - **Prizes per happy meter** 2–8 (4).
  - **Dance length** 5–15 s (9).
  - **Beat tempo** 80–140 BPM (109).
  - **On-beat window** ±0.05–0.25 s (±0.13).
  - **Tap delay allowance** 0–0.2 s (0.05): it moves the window later, for phone touch delay.
  - **Rain-coin chance** 0–50% (12%).
  - **Dance move** paw drum / sway / big paws (paw drum).
  - **Dance defaults** resets them all.
- **Sounds:** all generated in code. They follow the sound setting and the **Pickup volume** dial. The beat is scheduled ahead on the audio clock, early by the phone's reported audio output delay, so it's heard when the indicator shows it.

## Tuning (shipped defaults)
| Dial | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Prizes per meter | 4 | A winning round collects 7 prizes, so 1 break, sometimes 2 with repeats |
| Music-note coin | 1 per round (2nd–5th drop) + 6% | "about one per round"; with prizes, about 2 breaks in a good round |
| Dance length / tempo | 9 s / 109 BPM | 16 beats; 109 BPM is the Dancing pose's own speed (one move per 2.2 s bar), so the pose and the music line up |
| On-beat window | ±0.13 s | About half of each beat counts. Forgiving, but tapping fast loses through the off-beat cost |
| Tap delay allowance | 0.05 s | Phone touch delay; tune on the phone |
| Rain-coin chance | 12% | About 2 rain coins in a full 16-beat break |
| Max bank | 16 coins + rain coins | A perfect break is worth about a rain 10 plus rain-coin extras |

## Limits and notes
- **No phone evidence yet.** Beat timing on the phone (audio delay, touch delay) is the main thing to test. If on-beat taps feel late or early, the **Tap delay allowance** and **On-beat window** dials fix it without a build.
- **Round timer pauses during the break** (as the brief proposed), and Turbo and the bonus-drop clock pause too. The board itself keeps moving: the pusher, rain already falling, and drops already in the air.
- **Prizes during a break** don't add to the meter (it is already full). A music-note coin collected during a break is used up without a second break.
- **A win during a break** ends the break, and anything banked is lost (the win's own dance and cascade take over).
- **The roar** is a new generated sound, not the cat's existing growl recording, so it follows the Pickup volume dial. The cat's lunge animation is the existing one.
- **The goal card** doesn't mention the meter or the note coin. There's no room above GO; the HUD and the "DANCE BREAK!" title teach it instead.
- **The music-note coin, meter, beat lane and bank** are new code-drawn placeholders.
