## HOTFIX in UAT 113: a stuck countdown after banking a coin (Nov 19)
**Published to UAT only: 2026.11.19 UAT 113 / APK7, numeric 20261119000024** (game-only, on UAT 112). Backup
`uat/backups/before-UAT113-20261119000024`. Cloud copy verified (same shared item, SHA ff7ff909…, 59,442,087 bytes).
Production untouched.

**The bug (in UAT 111 and 112; not in production or UAT 110):** bank a side-game coin, then go Home and open another cat's
board, or start a new round: its 3-2-1 countdown never finishes.
- Cause: the slot's "just banked" pulse measured time from the game clock, which restarts on a new board or round. The time
  went negative, the pulse ring got a negative radius, and drawing threw on every frame, so the round never started.
- Found while checking another report: the same player steps in real Chrome stick on UAT 111 but reach play on UAT 110 and
  production.
- **Fix** (`work/cp113/`, the build name plus three in-place edits to the CP-88 block): a pulse from before the clock
  restarted counts as over, and the pulse is cleared on round reset and board exit. Nothing else changes.

**Checks (`tickets/CP-88-evidence/uat113/`):**
- Real Chrome, a player's steps (bank, Home, open Orange): UAT 112 freezes (`real-browser-stuck-uat112.json`); UAT 113 reaches
  play (`real-browser-stuck.json`).
- `checks88-stuck` / check 7 in `checks88-on113`: bank a coin; Home, another cat's board, and a new round all reach play, drawn
  every frame, on all three coin boards. The harness's canvas allows a negative radius, so the check makes it throw, as a
  browser does. It passes on UAT 113 and hits the negative radius on UAT 112.
- `checks88-on113` 8/8 (against UAT 110, before CP-88), `checks64` 10/10 (the frozen pickup identical), `checks112` 13/13 (the
  Hug), and the real-browser slot check (`real-browser-boot.json`).
- The rest of the regression set runs after publishing.

## What it does
Jenny, Nov 19: "While I ran Sonic Meow, the laser went over the edge and the laser game started on a board that had just been
cleared by Sonic Meow... Doesn't it seem like you should be able to save that coin and spend it when you're ready?"
Go: "CP-88 Bank side-game coins: laser, fish and mouse coins go to a tap-to-start slot instead of starting at once. Dance
break unchanged. Next UAT."
- **Banking:** the coin's own over-the-edge handler still runs, so the frozen pickup (look, timing, sound) is untouched. The
  side-game start it queues becomes one charge in the slot instead.
- **Tapping:** it starts the side game by queueing the same start the coin used to queue, so the side game's own start
  conditions and behaviour apply unchanged. The tap is refused (the power button's lock sound) unless the round is in play
  with no power (title or action), no Dance break, no wheel's Yawn, no side game or pending start, no paw swipe and no pause.
  Tapping is the only way to start them; the Developer "start now" buttons still work as before.
- **Charges last for the round.** A new round, or leaving the board, clears them, as the coin always belonged to its round.
  Carrying them across rounds would need saving, like power charges; it isn't done.
- **The slot:** 34 units, right of the Quake button and just below the round's Home button (Home sits on the power buttons'
  row there), in the cat power button's style at 0.82 scale (purple rings with the
  coin's colour, the coin's own sprite, a count badge). It is hidden while its side game runs. On the Siamese board the
  "LASER COIN SOON / LASER ×n" hint moves right of it (its dot is dropped, because the slot shows the coin).
- Source: `work/cp111/cp88-bank.js`; one edit (the laser hint's position) in the `build.py` that `setup.py` writes.
