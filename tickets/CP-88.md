## Phone test (UAT 111)
On the **Orange** (fish coin), **Siamese** (laser coin) and **Tortie** (mouse coin) boards:
1. A new round button sits right of the lightning (Quake) button, just below Home. It's in the power button's style and shows the board's coin
   with a count, starting at 0 (dimmed).
2. Push the side-game coin over the edge. Its pickup plays exactly as before, but the side game does **not** start. The new
   button pulses and its count goes up by 1. Two coins make 2.
3. Tap the button: the side game starts as it always has, and the count goes down by 1. While the side game runs, the button
   is hidden (the Siamese's laser timer bar shows there as before), and it comes back afterwards.
4. Tap it during a power (also during its title), the Dance break, the wheel's Yawn or another side game: you hear the lock
   sound, nothing starts and nothing is used. Tap again once that has ended: it starts.
5. Unused coins don't carry into the next round: a new round starts at 0.
6. The Tuxedo (Dance break) and the Ragdoll have no new button; the Dance break works as before.

## What it does
Jenny, Nov 19: "While I ran Sonic Meow, the laser went over the edge and the laser game started on a board that had just been
cleared by Sonic Meow... Doesn't it seem like you should be able to save that coin and spend it when you're ready?"
Go: "CP-88 Bank side-game coins: laser, fish and mouse coins go to a tap-to-start slot instead of starting at once. Dance
break unchanged. Next UAT."
- **Banking:** the coin's own over-the-edge handler still runs, so the frozen pickup (look, timing, sound) is untouched. The
  side-game start it queues becomes one charge in the slot instead.
- **Tapping:** it starts the side game by queueing the same start the coin used to queue, so the side game's own start
  conditions and behaviour apply unchanged. The tap is refused (the power button's lock sound) unless the round is in play
  with no power (title or action), no Dance break, no wheel's Yawn, no side game or pending start, no paw swipe and no pause.
  Tapping is the only way to start them; the Developer "start now" buttons still work as before.
- **Charges last for the round.** A new round, or leaving the board, clears them, as the coin always belonged to its round.
  Carrying them across rounds would need saving, like power charges; it isn't done.
- **The slot:** 34 units, right of the Quake button and just below the round's Home button (Home sits on the power buttons'
  row there), in the cat power button's style at 0.82 scale (purple rings with the
  coin's colour, the coin's own sprite, a count badge). It is hidden while its side game runs. On the Siamese board the
  "LASER COIN SOON / LASER ×n" hint moves right of it (its dot is dropped, because the slot shows the coin).
- Source: `work/cp111/cp88-bank.js`; one edit (the laser hint's position) in the `build.py` that `setup.py` writes.
