# CP-47 Levels and cats

## Freeze investigation (UAT 62, Nov 15): read-only, no build
**Short answer.** The page itself stopped (hung or the renderer died) on the phone right after Jenny flooded the board to about 130 coins. It is not a stuck button, overlay or power. It happened in all 3 logged sessions that reached about 130 coins, including one in an old mode before levels existed, so it is not caused by CP-47's levels or by Hug. **Proposed fix:** cap the coins on the board, plus native crash and stall handling. The exact cause (hang or renderer crash) still needs one answer from Jenny and better logging.

**What the logs show.** I read `uat/CatCoin-UAT-performance.json` and `uat/diagnostics/…`, four sessions in all.
- **The 18:59Z session (97 min, the freeze):**
  - Level 6 as the Ragdoll. Hug used at 5776 s and removed on time 6 s later.
  - She held almost non-stop and dropped all 110 coins in about 30 s. Coins on the board went from 53 to 129.
  - The phone's physics cost went from 3 ms to 45–60 ms per frame, pinned at the 3-steps-per-frame cap. The game ran at about 14 fps and the round clock at 0.7× speed.
  - The bank was empty from about 5803 s. Her last hold (5807–5809 s) dropped nothing.
  - The last save was at 5810.9 s, at about 36 s of game time, with about 24 s left on the clock. Turbo Pusher was due at 25 s left.
- **Then nothing more was ever saved.** Leaving the app always forces a save (`onPause` → `diagNativePause` → `diagFlush`, which works in the other sessions). She did leave: the next session starts at 21:50Z. So no JavaScript ran again after about 5811 s. The game didn't keep running with dead buttons: frames, taps and the exit save all stopped.
- **Same signature twice more** in the other file, both UAT 62:
  - 23:22Z: towers mode, 132 coins, physics up to 56 ms.
  - 03:13Z: 132 coins, physics up to 47 ms.
  - Each ends right after a hold during a flood, with no exit save. Each session was only about 1 minute long.
- The 21:50Z session is a normal relaunch: it sat on the ready screen, then went to the background.

**Desktop reproduction (`work/cp47-levels/freeze.cjs`, `leak.cjs`): no hang on desktop.**
- I replayed her last 40 s: Ragdoll, Level 6, Hug, then holds at her x positions until the bank was empty.
  - It reaches 131 coins on the board and 16–20 ms per tick (versus 1.6 ms at the start). That matches the phone's ×10, but desktop has 3–4× the headroom.
  - Turbo does not spike: about 18 ms per tick before, during and after it.
  - The round ends on time, not on coins, because coins keep trickling off the front. TRY AGAIN and Main screen both work afterwards. No frame errors.
- Without Hug, and on Level 1, the results are the same (127–131 coins, about 17 ms per tick). So the flood alone causes the load.
- 20 rounds across all levels, cats and powers: nothing builds up. Colliders, bodies and idle cost return to their Level 1 values, so the 97-minute length isn't the cause.

**Likely cause.** The phone's WebView renderer is pinned at 100% by about 130 coins being pushed (physics 50 ms plus drawing 16 ms in every 70 ms frame, sustained). That load ends in a renderer hang or crash.
- The app has no `onRenderProcessGone` handler and no watchdog, so we can't tell which. A crash normally closes the app. A hang leaves the last picture frozen, which is what she describes.
- Not ruled out: one runaway physics step on the phone (for example when Turbo starts while the board is jammed). Desktop did not show it.
- **One question for Jenny:** after it froze, did the picture stay on screen until she closed the app, or did the app close by itself? And did any sound keep playing?

**Proposed fix (for a ticket; needs PM and Jenny's OK):**
1. **Coins-on-board cap (game-only).** Above about 100 solid coins (normal play stays well under; the flood reached 129–132), a hold stops dropping. A short "Board full" hint shows, and drops resume when coins fall off. This removes the overload at its source.
   - It touches the hold/drop feel, not the frozen pickup. The PM should still check it against the keep list.
   - The softer alternative is to slow the drop rate as the count rises.
2. **Native, needs APK 8:**
   - Handle `onRenderProcessGone`: write a "renderer gone" note to the log file and reload the game instead of dying.
   - A watchdog: JS sends a heartbeat every second. If none arrives for 5 s while the app is in the foreground, write a "stall" note natively, and offer a reload after 15 s.

**What the log needs to capture next time (game-only, could ride with fix 1):**
- **Save sooner:**
  - Save at once on the first frame error. Today an error waits for the next hold-end save and is lost if the page dies first.
  - Keep saving every 5 s during play, even while holding. Today saves are skipped while holding, so the final seconds of a flood are lost.
- **More detail:**
  - A `long-frame` event for frames over 300 ms, with physics ms, steps, coins, bodies and colliders.
  - Keep 200 events, not 40.
  - The last 10 taps, each with what took it (wild/levels screen, power, drop, end card, nothing) and a UI-state snapshot: phase, board status, screen, power active, side game, panel/inventory open, holding.
  - Peak coins on the board for each round.
- **With fix 2, a native note** of "last JS heartbeat at …" and "renderer gone: crashed or killed". That finally tells a hang from a crash.

## UAT 61: levels are the boards, cats are who you play as (Jenny, Nov 14)
**Published: 2026.11.14 UAT 61 / APK7 (20261114235955). Waiting for Jenny's phone test.** Backup `uat/backups/before-UAT61-20261114235955`. A fresh cloud download matches what was published (SHA 7d758989…, 51,953,498 bytes, etag `…},97`, same shared item). Checkpoint `048b4f0` on `wip/uat`. Production unchanged.

One build (no split needed): levels, cats as characters, unlocks off levels, the five powers and the Tortoiseshell's new side game. Built on published UAT 60 (pinned by hash); production untouched.

### The levels
Fixed layouts in sequence, made from what exists. Every replay starts the same: the same prizes in the same spots, the same towers, rain coins and Quake, the same coins and time. The physics settle leaves pieces within about a tenth of a unit, which you can't see. Passing a level opens the next. "Level n" is this number everywhere: the PLAY screen, the end card, Developer. Each goal uses everyday prizes only (egg, milk, sardine, fish biscuit, feather, plus three new code-drawn ones: toy ball, box, sock). Extra copies on a board are bonus prizes.

| Level | Layout | Goal | Tag | Bot test (wins, time left) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 First push | Wild 2's two towers, 4 prizes near the front, Quake | 4 prizes | Normal | 4/4, ~25 s left |
| 2 Line up | 5 prizes in a row across the middle | 5 | Normal | 4/4, ~43 s |
| 3 At the back | No towers, 5 prizes at the back; 90 coins, 50 s, no Quake | 5 | Hard | 3/6, 0–18 s |
| 4 Crammed | 12 prizes packed in | 6 | Normal | 4/4, ~30 s |
| 5 Tower wall | The Tower wall, a prize on each tower | 5 | Hard | 3/4 |
| 6 Coin rain | 6 prizes and four rain coins (a 25 among them) | 6 | Normal | 4/4, ~40 s |
| 7 Edge of the table | Prizes on the side edges and far back; 80 coins, no Quake | 7 | Super hard | 1/6 |
| 8 Wall and back | Tower wall plus a prize behind it; 120 coins, 70 s | 6 | Hard | 2/6 |
| 9 Prize party | 14 prizes, no towers | 5 | Normal | 4/4, ~40 s |
| 10 The vault | Tower wall, 3 prizes behind it in the back corners; 65 coins, 55 s | 8 | Super hard | 0/6 |

The difficulty doesn't climb steadily. Easy levels sit between hard ones, and 7 and 10 are walls you can be stuck on. The bot drops a coin every 0.45 s, sweeping across the board, and uses Quake. It doesn't aim or use Paw save, so you should do better than it. The tags follow its results: Normal = won easily; Hard = about half won, with little time left; Super hard = almost never. Frames: `CP-47-evidence/uat61/levels-sheet.png`.

**PLAY screen (layout A, unchanged otherwise):**
- The cat's name.
- "LEVEL n" and its tag.
- Small stars for that level once earned.
- "‹ ›" arrows to replay a passed level and come back to the newest.
- The goal line names the level ("Crammed: collect these 6").

**End card:** "Level 3 passed! Level 4 is open · +25 coins". The big button reads NEXT LEVEL after a first pass and TRY AGAIN after a loss. The small one is "Main screen". Stars (1–3) are kept per level.

**No help, no rigging:** after a loss the level starts exactly as before. No free Quake, nothing moved (checked). Unchanged from UAT 60: a goal prize lost off a side still comes back as a bonus drop.

### Each cat's package
| Cat | Side game | Power (earned by) | Its prizes (sky drops, only while you play it) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuxedo | Dance break | Yawn (a perfect Dance break) | Bow tie, music notes |
| Orange | Treat toss | Zoomies (catch every treat) | Tuna, yarn |
| Ragdoll | Pet the cat | Hug (a perfect peak purr) | Cheese, cushion |

- **The level's layout stays whichever cat you bring** (checked on all ten). The side game, power and prizes come with the cat.
- **A cat's prizes are never in a level's layout.** Two fall from the sky each round (about 10 s and 32 s after GO), and about half the other bonus prize drops are the cat's too. They show their name ("TUNA!").
- **Shared on every level:** Quake and the rain coins, Paw save. The wheel still comes from a perfect Dance break (Tuxedo).

**Mouse pounce (proposal, built):**
- Push the mouse coin off (one of the bonus drops, as with the fish coin).
- For about 8 s, 14 toy mice scurry across the board, a few at first, then faster, with some darting and pairs late on.
- Tap one and the mitt slams down on it (+3 coins to the bank).
- A tap on nothing is a miss (red cross, bonk). A mouse that gets away gets a small cross.
- The round timer holds. A win waits until the pounce ends.
- At the end the bank pours down as a coin rain. Every mouse caught = PERFECT!: a RAIN 10 and a Tortitude.
- One gesture (tap), every tap a hit or a miss. Frames: `pounce-play.png`, `pounce-end.png`.

### Getting cats
The chain is unchanged: recipe → cook → Porch visit → photo → that cat's coin drops on any level → push it off.
- The coin now makes the cat playable: "ORANGE JOINS YOU!".
- Once you have two cats, the **Cats** button (where Boards was) opens **PICK A CAT**. It shows each cat's side game, power, prizes and power count, and it's usable between rounds.
- There is no level-5 gate.

### Powers
- **Earning:** each power is earned by playing the cat's side game perfectly, and it arrives once the side game ends ("YAWN +1!").
- **The button:** it sits beside Quake, in the slot the paw-sweep used (empty on these boards). It shows the playing cat's power and count, up to 9. Tap to use it, on any level, while you play that cat.
- **Sounds:** every power is loud with the game's existing sounds. No new audio, so CP-46 isn't touched.

| Power | What happens | Frame |
|---|---|---|
| Yawn | The cat stretches and swipes things off the sides (the wheel's Yawn) | `power-yawn.png` |

### What to try (UAT 61)
1. **PLAY: Level 1 · Normal, "First push: collect these 4".** Win it: the end card says "Level 1 passed! Level 2 is open". NEXT LEVEL plays Level 2.
2. **Lose a level**, then play it again: same layout, same start.
3. **Pass Level 2:** the Kitchen button appears with the Tuna dish; the Shop appears for the tuna.
4. **During a round as the Tuxedo:** a bow tie or music notes drop from the sky ("BOW TIE!").
5. **Powers and Mouse pounce:** Settings → Developer → Gameplay map → "Give all cat coins", then "Give 3 of every power". Use Cats to switch cats, and tap the power button beside Quake in a round. "Start Mouse pounce now" tries the Tortoiseshell's game.
6. **The hard ones:** Developer → "Open every level", then try Levels 7 and 10 from the PLAY screen's ‹ › arrows.
