### What it is
Home > **Endless board** (UAT; under Board lab, or in its place when Developer is off). It plays the cat you're on, as PLAY
does, on an open board: no round timer, no goal row, no "2 / 5" counter, no win or level complete. Home always leaves.
- **Coin wallet**: start **100**, **+1 every 30 s** of real time (app open or closed), **cap 100** (empty to full in 50 min).
  Kept on the phone between sessions. Coins off the front go back into the wallet as always (a coin off the front always counts;
  they may take it over the cap, and the refill then waits). The clock spot shows the refill ("+1 0:23" or "Full"). Empty
  wallet: no drops, "0 coins", the clock counting down, and "Out of coins · +1 in 0:23" on the board.
- **Reset meter**: the bar above the cat's head, "New board 30%". It fills by coins played, **200 drops**; full = a green
  **NEW BOARD** button. A tap (refused with the lock sound while a power, side game, Yawn or Dance break runs) sweeps the board:
  a bar crosses from the back to the front and shoves everything off, **paying nothing**. Then the next setup is built and a
  "GO!" shows. The meter fills at once when the board is nearly empty (**fewer than 20 coins** on the table for 1.5 s; boards
  start with 31-63).
- **Board order**: the 10 level layouts in level order, then the 11 lab layouts (tower, edge, pins, wall, mice, red, lanes, cup,
  bumper, gate, chain). First pass in that order; then random from the seen boards, never the same one twice running. A board
  added to the list later is next in line. Each layout keeps its own prizes (and the bonus drops still bring back its lost
  ones), but nothing is shown as a goal. Level 1's quiet rule (no bonus prizes) doesn't apply here.
- **Side-game coins** (PM, Nov 19): the cat's own coin (laser on the Siamese, fish on the Orange, mouse on the Tortie) comes as
  the next bonus drop once **100 coins** have been played since the last one (today's levels give about one per 65-120-coin
  round). Never while one is on the board, falling in, pending, or its game is running. A coin lost off a side brings the next
  after 50. It lands in the front part of the board (depth 600-680). **Fish and mouse start their game at once** (as before
  CP-88; their slot is hidden here). **The laser is banked** in the CP-88 slot and **kept with the wallet** (sessions, new
  boards, leaving) until used.
- Kept as on a level: prizes to the pantry with the frozen pickup, the cat's power and side game, bonus drops, rain, the wheel.
- Levels unchanged: their clock, goals, wins, and CP-88 banking reset per round, exactly as today.
- Developer: Settings > Engineer tools > "Endless board": Empty wallet, Fill wallet, Fill meter, Start order again.

### Numbers (headless real game, `work/cp92-endless/measure.cjs`; logs in `work/cp92-endless/logs/`)
- **Return per 100 coins played (natural, no adjustment):** mean **102.5** over 6 boards (First push 107.7, Crammed 96.3, Tower
  block 106.0, Prize party 100.3, Bowling pins 108.7, Cup shot 95.7; Tuxedo, 300 drops after 150 to settle, one every 0.3 s
  across the lane). With the cats' side games: First push (Siamese) 93.3, Crammed (Orange) 105.6, Prize party (Tortie) 106.2.
  So the wallet about breaks even on an ordinary board; it doesn't run down by itself. Of that, "REPEAT +5 COINS" (a repeat
  prize pays 5 coins, the levels' rule) is 6-12 per 100.
- **Side-game coin:** arrived every 100-140 coins played (the bonus clock pauses during side games). Coins played from landing
  to going over the front: laser (First push) 10, 43, 95, 47; fish (Crammed) 81, 23, 57; mouse (Prize party) 18, 28, 31, 28.
  Typical **about 30-45**; none lost off a side.
- **To bring the return to about 90 later (proposals, not built; visible levers only):**
  1. On the endless board, a repeat prize goes to the pantry like any prize instead of paying +5 coins (cuts about 6-12 per
     100; simple; there's no collection to repeat on an open board).
  2. Short gaps in the side rails near the front, so some coins drop off the sides where the player can see it (real pushers
     do this); size them to the measured shortfall.

### Limits
- The board itself isn't saved: coming back rebuilds the board you were on (wallet, meter, laser charges and the order are kept).
- The empty-wallet text on the board is the small status line; the big cue is "0 coins" and the refill clock at the top.
- Switching cat on the board rebuilds the same layout. Recipes, visiting cats, prize odds and cat switching are later tickets.
- Phone feel, sound and FPS need the phone.
