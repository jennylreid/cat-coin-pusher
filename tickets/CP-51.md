# CP-51 Drop speed and Pause — engineer report (UAT 68, Nov 16, 2026)

**Published to UAT only: 2026.11.16 UAT 68 / APK7, numeric 20261116180000** (game-only, on UAT 67; CP-50's work unchanged).
Backup `uat/backups/before-UAT68-20261116180000`; fresh anonymous cloud download matches (sha256 `f6edcf66…`, 51,990,814
bytes, same OneDrive item, etag 104). Production untouched (`phone/CatCoinPusher-game.json` hash unchanged). Checkpoint
`ff7b1b3` on `wip/uat`, pushed. Source `work/cp68/build.py` + `cp51.js` on pinned UAT 67; checks `work/cp68/checks68.cjs`
(25/25); evidence `tickets/CP-51-evidence/uat68/` (screens at all four sizes in `shots/`, UAT 67 in `before-uat67/`, bot
runs in `bot/`). Includes the brief's section 3 add-ons (Level 1 intro board, quieter prize bell).

## 1. Drop speed
**When it went up:** CP-25 UAT 3 (Oct 28, `work/cp25/stage2`), when hold to drop arrived. Production before that (Oct 27)
had tap only; CP-25 UAT 2 gated taps at 0.35 s. From UAT 3 on, holding and tapping shared one 0.20 s gap
(`DROP_INTERVAL`), and nothing changed it after that.

| | Old (UAT 3 → UAT 67) | UAT 68 |
|---|---|---|
| Holding | a coin every 0.20 s (5 a second) | **every 0.40 s (2.5 a second)** |
| Tapping | up to 5 a second; early taps dropped nothing ("Easy taps") | **up to one every 0.25 s (4 a second)**; one early tap waits and drops when the gap opens, so fast taps aren't lost |

- The first coin of every press still drops at once.
- **Why 0.40 s:** it halves the stream and is close to the old 0.35 s tap gate. At 2.5 a second, a 60 s round uses about
  150 coins against a 110-coin bank, so a hold still gets through the bank. 0.50 s was also tried: peaks and wins were
  much the same (18/30 vs 19/30), so the faster of the two was kept.
- Measured with real pointer events over 6 s: hold 5.17 → 2.67 a second; taps at 5 a second 5 → 4.17; taps at 3 a
  second unchanged (3).

## 2. The 45-coin limit: retired
- UAT 68 has no own-coin limit. The only net is the far-off backstop: at **120 coin pieces** (plain coins plus gold 5s),
  drops slow to one a second with "Board full". This is CP-48's own slow-down, now counting pieces and starting much
  further out.
- I first tried a hard stop at 120. Non-stop tapping then won 11/30 rounds (19/30 on UAT 67), so I kept the slow-down.
- **A full-length hold still fills the hard boards.** On Levels 3-8 and 10, a non-stop sweeping hold reaches the
  backstop: coins arrive faster than they fall off, even at 2.5 a second. "Board full" time drops a lot, though:
  - hold: 41 s a round on average on UAT 67, 13 s on UAT 68;
  - fast tapping: 36 s a round on UAT 67, 21 s on UAT 68.

## 3. Pause
- **Button:** a soft pill around the round timer: clock, time, and two pause bars. It's in the top bar, clear of the
  powers, Home, the gear and the Dance meter, and only shows in a round in play. TURBO's countdown moved 8 px right to
  sit beside it.
- **Tapping it stops:** the timer, physics, the pusher, drops, any hold, side games and the music. The dance's beat clock
  shifts by the pause, so a paused dance picks up in time. A prize pickup in the air stays on screen and finishes after
  Resume; the pickup itself is unchanged.
- **Pop-up** (end-card style):
  - **RESUME**.
  - **FINISH ROUND:** the normal end card with "ROUND OVER". Prizes go to the inventory and coins to the jar, as in a
    lost round (0 stars); it's a win if every prize is already in.
  - **Sound: On/Off**.
  - **How to play:** the "? How to play" goal card, with BACK in place of TAP TO START.
  - **Home:** does exactly what the round's top Home button does.
- **Auto-pause:** when the app goes to the background or the screen turns off (native `appPaused`), or the page is
  hidden. The pop-up is there on return.
