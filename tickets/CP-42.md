## What was built
- **The mode:** **LASER CHASE** in the MODES picker, registered with CP-41's `registerWildMode()` (`features:{laser:true}`). No new header button, no picker of its own (the PM's call).
  - It is Wild 2: the goal card with TAP TO START (goal: collect all 7 prizes), the 60 s timer, rain coins, bonus drops, towers, Turbo, music, the win and lose cards, the star prize and the visitor.
  - It has no happy meter, dance break or music-note coin. Its side game is Laser chase.
  - Goal card lines: the laser coin, drag the red dot, pounce near the front, then Wild 2's lines.
- **The laser coin:** code-drawn, dark grey with a red ring and a glowing red dot, with "LASER" on it. It has the same physics as a rain coin.
  - It is always the **2nd bonus drop** (marker, whistle, "LASER!"), unless a lost prize needs replacing first. It lands near the front (depth 625–690), so it goes over in a few pushes.
  - A second one comes later only if the first never went over (in 6 rounds with no coins dropped, the count was 1 or 2 each time).
  - Push it off and it pops like a rain coin. Laser chase starts as the pop is shown.
- **Laser chase (9 s by default):**
  - **Start:** a laser "zip" and a chirp, "LASER CHASE!" and "Drag the red dot. The cat pounces!" (0.9 s), then the dot appears on the board.
  - **The dot:** touch anywhere and the dot jumps to your finger (a tiny "tik" and a red ring); drag and it follows. It stays on the table.
  - **While the chase runs:** every touch moves the dot, and no coins drop. EXIT and Settings still work. If you never touch, "Drag the dot!" blinks beside it.
  - **The paw:** the mitt on the dot's side (the approved art, moved and tilted in code, never redrawn) leaves its armrest and chases the dot, lifted with a shadow under it.
    - When it's on the dot it wiggles (a rustle) and leaps (a whoosh). It slams down where the dot was when it locked on, so a dot that moves at the last moment escapes.
    - That's about 8–9 pounces in a chase. The other mitt stays at rest, and both go back when the chase ends.
  - **The cat watches the dot:** the pupils follow it (the game's own eye tracking), and the head leans a little toward it.
  - **Each pounce says what it hit:**
    - **SWAT!** (gold) on a prize: thump, coin clinks, a bright "ding-ding", gold ring, sparks, a double buzz.
    - **BAP!** / **BAP BAP!** on coins: thump plus one clink per coin, a white ring and dust.
    - **miss** on bare table: a soft whiff and a small grey cross.
  - **The swat:** everything within 58 units of the pads is thrown toward the front, a little outward and up. It's stronger in the middle; prizes get half a coin's push, towers about half.
    - The game normally caps coin speed every step (soft landings). Swatted things fly free for 0.35 s.
  - **Knocked off:** whatever the paw hit and that goes over the front, during the chase or up to 1.6 s after, gets a "+1" and a rising ding. It's collected normally (prizes Pop and count toward the goal; rain coins rain).
  - **The banner** (where Wild 2 shows the happy meter): a red bar for the chase time left, and "×N" knocked off. The round timer shows the pause mark. Outside a chase it reads "LASER COIN SOON", then "LASER ×N" for the round's total.
  - **End:** **"+N KNOCKED OFF!"** with pounces, prize swats and prizes off underneath, then a fanfare and confetti. If nothing went over: "NOTHING FELL!" and a soft two-note fall.
- **Timer and wins:** the round timer, Turbo and the bonus-drop clock pause for the whole chase. A win during the chase waits until it ends, then the win card and the visitor come as usual. A loss or EXIT ends it at once.
- **Paw save (CP-40)** is off while a chase runs, because the mitts are busy and every touch moves the dot. A chase never starts in the middle of a save swipe.
- **Developer dials** (Settings → Developer mode → Laser chase). They are saved on the phone, and the performance log carries `laserTuning`, `laserTuningDefaults` and `laserLog` (the last 3 chases: every dot jump, pounce with what it hit, and item over the front):
  - **Start laser chase now**: opens Laser mode if needed and starts a chase after GO, or straight away in a running round.
  - **Chase length** 5–15 s (9).
  - **Paw speed** 0.5–2× (1): chase speed, wiggle and the gap between pounces.
  - **Swat strength** 0.5–2× (1).
  - **Laser chase defaults** resets them.

## Limits and open points
- **The paw's size:** it is the approved mitt at its normal size (182 px), so it covers a fair patch of the board while it hovers. That reads as "big cat paw", but say if it should be smaller in flight (a transform, no redraw).
- **No phone evidence yet:** drag feel, touch latency, sound over the music and FPS. Wild 2 already runs at 11–17 FPS on her phone; a chase adds physics like a burst of drops.
- **Prizes:** a skilled drag knocks 2–3 prizes off in one chase, so the chase can carry much of the goal. The Swat strength dial (and prizes getting half a coin's push) are the levers. Treat toss caps goal prizes at 2.
- **Coins at the lip:** on UAT 45, a coin can rest at the front lip for a while. The laser coin was moved forward and made the 2nd bonus drop so it reliably goes over in a 60 s round.
- **Mode access:** I first prototyped my own "MODES ▾" pill. The PM asked for one picker, so the published build uses CP-41's registry. The pill code stays in `laser46.js` only for builds without the registry (it does not show in UAT 46).
- **Mode goal and cat:** the goal stays Wild 2's (collect all 7 prizes), and the Tuxedo runs it. The design doc lists Laser mode's goal as open.
