### What it does (endless board only)
- **The chip** under the cat's chin (right) now has a small ▾. Tap it: a row of the cats' **approved faces** (as drawn on Home;
  no new art) opens just below; the cat playing is ringed. Only the cats you have are shown.
- **Tap a face**: that cat takes over at once on the same board (no fade, no rebuild): its art, its side game, its power and
  power button, its side-game coin rule. The board pieces, coins, wallet, reset meter, board order and banked laser coin stay
  exactly as they are. A tap anywhere else closes the row and drops nothing.
- **Locked** (the chip dims and shows a padlock; a tap gives the lock sound and a little shake, and the row doesn't open):
  while a power runs (its title, action and held beat), a side game runs or is about to start, the Dance break or its wheel,
  the Jackpot, a side-game coin (music note, fish, laser, heart, mouse) is on the board or falling in, the NEW BOARD sweep, a
  Quake, a Paw save swipe, or the countdown. It unlocks by itself after. An open row closes if something locks it.
- **No farming:** each cat's own per-board counters (its side-game coin schedule, the Dance meter) are kept when you switch
  away and restored when you come back on the same board, so switching can't bring a coin again early. A cat new to this board
  starts fresh, counted from the board's bonus drops so far (its first coin comes on the usual schedule). A new board clears this.
- Timed levels: the chip is a tag as today; tapping it does nothing.
