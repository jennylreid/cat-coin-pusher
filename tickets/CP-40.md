# CP-40 Paw save (Wild 2)

## Phase 1 findings (Nov 12, engineer; desktop runs, prototype on a copy of UAT 42, nothing published)

Planned for UAT 44 on top of CP-24 tipping (`tickets/CP-24.md`), which makes prizes actually tip over the side edges. Phase 2 applies both on UAT 43.

### How it works (prototype `work/cp44-bug/pawsave40.js`, full build `work/cp44-bug/prototype-uat44.html`)
- **Tip and glow:** on Wild 2 only, a prize that tips over an open side edge, centre of mass past the edge, hangs and glows for 0.5 s.
  - The glow is a pulsing gold halo with a rising "uh-oh" (two rising notes).
  - Without the hold, a falling prize leaves the board in about 0.15 s, too fast to tap. The prize sinks slowly during the window so it reads as "about to go".
- **Tap on the glowing prize** (within 64 units of it): uses one save.
  - The cat's mitt slides out to the prize: the approved mitt art, unchanged, with the yawn's easing. It flicks once and slides back, for about 0.56 s in all.
  - The flick sends the prize back over the edge and up, so it lands on the board. In the test it landed about 78 units inside the edge.
  - Then a swipe sound (whoosh, thump and a little chime) and a green "SAVED!" pop.
- **Tap anywhere else:** does what taps normally do, drops a coin, and uses no save.
- **Window ends without a save:** the prize falls normally, into UAT 43's lost-prize moment (sad plop, "aww", "Lost the X!") once on top of UAT 43.
- **No saves left:** no glow; the prize just falls.
- **Saves per round:** 3. The HUD shows them as three small copies of the mitt art under "Collect all 7!", dimmed as they're used ([HUD](CP-40-evidence/cp40-hud-saves.png)). They reset each round.
- **Developer → "Paw save (Wild 2)":**
  - "Force a tip-off now" opens Wild 2 if needed and places a prize you still need hanging over a side edge.
  - "Paw saves per round" dial, 0–9, saved on the device.
- **Other boards:** Boards 1–3 and Wild are unchanged; no glow and no saves there.

### Evidence (desktop)
- **Sequence:** glow, mitt slides in, flick, SAVED!, back on the board ([frames](CP-40-evidence/cp40-paw-save-sequence.png)).
- **Checks** (`work/cp44-bug/save40.cjs`, [output](CP-40-evidence/checks.json)):
  - Tap on the glow: 3 → 2 saves, the prize is back on the table, no coin dropped.
  - Tap elsewhere: a coin drops and the saves stay at 3. The window lasted 29 frames (0.48 s), then the prize fell.
  - Saves run out: 2, 1, 0; the fourth tip-off doesn't glow.
  - Wild (not Wild 2): no glow.
- **How often it comes up:** with CP-24, a prize goes off a Wild 2 side about once a minute in the test play (4 in 5 rounds). So 3 saves per round should rarely run out unless the board gets messy.

### Limits and open points
- Desktop only. Tap feel, sound level and whether 0.5 s is long enough on the phone need Jenny's test. The window and tap radius are constants in the prototype; Phase 2 can add them as dials.
- **Prototype sounds:** generated tones, played at the pickup volume. They're placeholders until Jenny hears them.
- **Mitt near the screen edge:** on the far side of a wide board the mitt slides partly off-screen while it reaches the prize.
- **Phase 2 on UAT 43:** check it alongside UAT 43's lost-prize moment, star prize (a saved star stays on the board) and gold "5" coins.
