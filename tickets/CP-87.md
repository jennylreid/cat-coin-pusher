## What it does (UAT only)
Jenny, Nov 18: "in the UAT build, make another button for it on the home screen"; "We can put all the games in there."
- Home shows a teal **Daily puzzles** button under PLAY. It opens a full-screen menu of the five prototypes: Paw Swat and Cat Café (CP-84), and Pocket Pounce, Moon Mint and Whisker Spark (CP-85).
- Each puzzle plays full screen under a bar with **‹ Puzzles** (back to the menu) and **Home**.
- The puzzle pages are embedded byte-for-byte (`work/cp107/pages.json` lists their hashes). The only addition is a small audio shim at the top of each page. With it, the app's Sound effects setting applies (sound off = puzzles silent), and a puzzle goes quiet while the app is in the background.
- While the puzzles are open, the game is paused (no frames run) and its sound is suspended. On return, Home carries on. Rounds, saves and settings are untouched. The puzzles keep their own `cp84-`/`cp85-` storage keys.
- Fixed while preparing: the tap that opens the menu could also press the puzzle card under the finger (Moon Mint opened by itself at 360x640). Taps in the first half-second after opening are now ignored.
- Size: one block of 266 KB (`work/cp107/cp87-puzzles.js`) appended to index.html. No new runtime files, and the update JSON grows by about 0.35 MB.
- Source: `work/cp107/cp87-shell.js` + `make-block.py` (embeds the pages) + `build.py` (`DEV=1` gives the scratch build on UAT 105).
