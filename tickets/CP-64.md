# CP-64 Jackpot cascade: report (engineer, Nov 17)

## Investigation (Nov 17): Dance breaks right after a Jackpot in UAT 89 (nothing built or published)
Jenny: "still often getting the dance beat side game right after jackpot". 36 runs on UAT 89 (harness, real game
script). Probe `work/cp89/probe-after-jackpot.cjs`, rows `tickets/CP-64-evidence/uat89/after-jackpot-runs.jsonl`.
Levels 2, 4 and 8, seeds 1-4, normal tapping, 40 s watched after the end.

**Paths (UAT 89):**
1. **Old prizes shoved off during the Jackpot (most likely "often").** Only prizes the Jackpot dropped are excluded.
   The Turbo pusher and ~45 drops also push the prizes that were already on the board over the front, and those
   fill the meter as normal. If it fills, the break is held and starts as soon as the Jackpot ends.
   - Level 4: 1-4 notches during every Jackpot (meter has 4).
   - "Start a Jackpot now" on Level 4: 2 of 4 runs filled it, so the Dance break started **0.2 s and 1.1 s after
     the end**.
   - Levels 2 and 8: rarely (0-1 notch), because those boards hold fewer prizes near the front.
2. **The music note is due straight after a Jackpot.** The round's one note coin replaces a bonus drop once enough
   bonus drops have come, and the Jackpot's ~45 drops count as bonus drops. So in 19 of 24 Jackpot runs, the
   **first bonus drop after the end (1.4-3.6 s) was the note**. If she collects it, the meter fills at once and a
   Dance break starts (seen at 5 s and 8.4 s after the end).
3. **A meter already full before the Jackpot** is held and starts its break at the end (as agreed). From the wheel
   this can't happen, because the meter is empty after the dance that spins the wheel. With the Developer button it
   can, if the meter was full.
4. **Not paths:** the cascade drops no music notes. The wheel's DANCE AGAIN is its own slice, not the Jackpot.
   Jackpot prizes themselves add 0 (UAT 89 check).
   - Her build matters: on **UAT 88 or earlier**, Jackpot prizes fill the meter, so a break follows **every**
     Jackpot.

**Numbers (time from the Jackpot's end to the next Dance break, within 40 s):**
| Start | Level 2 | Level 4 | Level 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheel → JACKPOT | none (4/4) | 6.6, 10.6, 12.2, 15.2 s | 8.4 s (note), 31 s (note), none, none |
| Developer button | none (4/4) | **0.2 s, 1.1 s**, 6.6, 16.7 s | 5 s (note), none x3 |
| Control: wheel → QUAKE (no Jackpot) | 22.7-32.1 s (4/4) | 9.5-19.6 s (4/4) | 5.6, 38 s, none x2 |

Normal play brings a Dance break about every 10-30 s anyway. Breaks within a few seconds of the end come from paths 1
and 2. On Level 4, path 1 also makes the next break come sooner (the meter is already at 1-2 when the Jackpot ends).

**Proposed fix (needs Jenny's go):**
- **A. Fix the cause (all levels):** while a Jackpot runs, no prize fills the meter, including old ones shoved off.
  Also, Jackpot drops don't count toward the music note. The meter stays where it was before the Jackpot, and a full
  one still gets its break at the end.
- **B. Jenny's beginner rule:** on **Levels 1-4**, no Dance break starts within **20 s** of a Jackpot ending. A meter
  that fills in that window waits, and its break starts at 20 s. From Level 5 on, back-to-back is allowed.
  - Why 20 s: in the runs above, normal play brings the next break 10-30 s after a wheel, so 20 s feels like an
    ordinary gap.
  - Why Level 4: it is where the old prizes push the meter hardest.
- A alone removes the "right after" cases. B guarantees a breather for beginners on top. Both are a few lines in the
  existing add-on block. The pickup is unchanged.

## What changed
- **The wheel's JACKPOT slice** (perfect-dance wheel) now starts a Jackpot cascade instead of 25 coins. The wheel reads
  "JACKPOT" and the result "JACKPOT!" (same slice, colour, coin icon and jingle).
- **The cascade** starts as soon as the Dance break ends: "JACKPOT!" and confetti, then prize after prize drops, with the
  usual bonus marker, whistle, "BONUS PRIZE!" and landing. The pusher runs at Turbo speed while it lasts, so the prizes
  spill over the front edge. Drops stop at 8.4 s. Once the last ones have landed (about 9 s in all) it shows
  "JACKPOT OVER". It's a timed state with its own end, and it always ends by 11 s.
- **The round timer pauses for it** (also Turbo, the bonus-drop clock and the out-of-coins clock), then resumes.
  Goal prizes it drops count as usual.

  right after GO, or straight away in a round already in play.

## Cascade mix and caps (my call)
- **One drop every 0.16 s** (about 6 a second) for 8.4 s, **at most 48 drops**.
- **Mix: 80% prizes, 10% Rain 5, 10% Quake** (at most 2 Quakes).
  - Prizes come from the round's collection.
  - The star prize only drops while it's missing, as in normal bonus drops.
  - On a crowded board (CP-61's 100 coins), a Rain 5 becomes a prize. Rain 10 and 25 never drop.
- **Where they land:** 70% near the front edge, kept away from the sides; 30% mid-board.
- **Caps:** no new drop while the board holds 210 pieces or 28 prizes. Pieces already on the board are never changed.
- **In the runs:** 42-48 drops, 35-42 of them prizes, 2-7 Rain 5s and 2 Quakes. The board peaked at 121-135 pieces
  (normal play reaches 160-200).
  - With 12 Jackpots back-to-back on Level 8, the peak was 163.
  - Real Chrome: 87-99 pieces.
- **Rule calls to confirm:**
  - **A win during the Jackpot waits for its end** ("ALL 5! JACKPOT!"), like a win during a Dance break. A Dance break
    that was waiting is then skipped, because the round is already won.
  - **A full Happy Cat meter during the Jackpot** (prizes fill it) waits, and its Dance break starts right after.
  - **END ROUND, Home or the round ending** stop the Jackpot at once. **Pause** freezes it.

## Limits
- Phone smoothness, sound load and the feel come only from her test. The tick numbers are desktop.
- Real Chrome ran in headless real time, which is not a phone.
- At about 2% of perfect-dance wheels, a player will rarely see a natural Jackpot. The Developer button is the way to
  test it.
- **Side losses:** with this many prizes, 5-9 go over the sides in a Jackpot. They are the normal lost prizes ("LOST
  THE …!", icon shake) and come back through the bonus drop, as in the CP-59 cascade. Losses of prizes she already has
  are silent. Narrowing the drops further is an option if it reads badly.
- Callouts share one slot, so a power or "COIN RAIN" callout can cover "JACKPOT!" for a moment (seen once in Chrome).
- Side games other than the Dance break (modes: toss, chase, pet, pounce) aren't held during a Jackpot. A Jackpot only
  comes from the Wild 2 wheel; the Developer button on a mode board would overlap them harmlessly (timers held twice).
