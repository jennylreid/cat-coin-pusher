# CP-85 · CatCoin daily puzzle prototypes

Three fixed boards per prototype, with tap controls, a shot limit, instant restart, and a sound toggle. Coins carry a suit as well as a colour. A large shot sends individual coins flying to the cat while the count climbs and the clinks resolve into a chord.

## Pocket Pounce

The cat fires the colours in a visible queue. Tap one of three pockets with a matching mouth.
The shot clears consecutive matching stacks until it hits a colour that blocks it.
When two pockets match, choose the one that exposes the colour you need next. Clear all pockets before the queue runs out.

- **Big payoff** — two Rose stacks of 12 and 13 clear together on the opening board. A single exposed coin can also be the right shot.
- **Page** — [Pocket Pounce](../work/cp85-puzzles/pocket-pounce.html) · [25-coin clip](CP-85-evidence/pocket-pounce-rush.mp4)
- **Improve** — test a longer forecast where both matching mouths look promising, and add a shot trail into the pocket to make the blocking rule more tangible.
- **Jenny** — fun / meh / no

## Moon Mint

Only the three bright windows expose the cat’s coin carousel. Tap Rose, Mint or Honey to fire through all three windows.
Every shot moves the carousel one slot, even if it misses. Choose when to catch matching stacks together before they pass under cover.
Clear the carousel within the shot limit.

- **Big payoff** — one Rose shot clears 8 + 12 + 5 coins across three windows on the opening board. Later boards make you earn the timing.
- **Page** — [Moon Mint](../work/cp85-puzzles/moon-mint.html) · [25-coin clip](CP-85-evidence/moon-mint-rush.mp4)
- **Improve** — try a visible track behind the windows and test a board where leaving a tempting match for a later revolution pays off.
- **Jenny** — fun / meh / no

## Whisker Spark

Tap any stack to fire from it along its row and column. All matching coins on those two lines clear, including across gaps.
A stack can connect two profitable lines. Spend it on a smaller shot and you can lose the best place to fire from.
Clear the rug within the shot limit.

- **Big payoff** — the opening Rose cross clears 25 coins. The final board has a Mint cross worth 38.
- **Page** — [Whisker Spark](../work/cp85-puzzles/whisker-spark.html) · [25-coin clip](CP-85-evidence/whisker-spark-rush.mp4)
- **Improve** — try a brief row-and-column glow before the coins fly so players can read exactly what the shot hit.
- **Jenny** — fun / meh / no
