## Causes

**1. Coins under the cat's face: they stack up the backstop above the pusher.**
The pusher's deck runs back under an invisible backstop at depth 190 (`LEDGE_BACK`) that is 300 units tall. Taps land
at depth 232 (`DROP_Y`). When the pusher pulls back, the coins on its deck are dragged against the backstop, and with
hold-to-drop every new coin lands on the last one. They build a column leaning on the backstop. On Board 3, 1.5 s of
holding already makes a column of 6 coins (heights 45 to 89), and on a busy board it reaches 7 to 14 coins (heights 60
to 116). The cat's head is painted after every coin, and its chin line is at depth 215. Any coin more than about two
high at the back is drawn up under the chin and face, so it vanishes. This is real on every board. It wasn't a
tunnelling problem, and the back-wall clip doesn't toggle (0 toggles measured).

**2. Flicker: the draw order of nearly touching coins swapped every frame.**
`coinPaintOrder` decides which of two overlapping coins is in front from the physics contact normal between them. When
two bodies nearly touch (any packed pile), that normal swings with sub-pixel jiggle, so the pair's order flipped from
frame to frame while neither moved. Example (Wild, seed 4, 5 s in): a mouse prize and a rain coin, both perfectly still
(position change 0.00), had a front/back value of −0.34, +0.13, −0.02, +0.11 on four frames running. Their order went
`BABABABBBBABBBBBBB` over 18 frames, and the mouse's ear blinks in front of and behind the coin
(`CP-37-evidence/flicker-pair-before.png`).
A second source on the Wild boards: the two **towers** had no order record at all, so where they fell among the coins
around them changed whenever anything else moved.

## Fix (UAT 39 = UAT 38 + this; same code on every board, Wild and Wild 2 included)

- **Coins can't climb the backstop into the face** (`work/cp39/fix37-backwall.js`). After each physics step, any coin,
  prize or rain coin whose drawn centre has risen above the chin line (CHIN_Y + 6) near the back gets slid forward at
  90 units/s until it's back in sight. In play it looks like the top of the pile tipping forward. It skips coins still
  falling from a tap, towers, paused play and the win dance's own copy of the coins. No random numbers; nothing else
  in the physics changes.
- **Steady draw order** (`work/cp39/fix37-order.js` plus four small edits in `coinPaintOrder`).
  - Each overlapping pair keeps its last order. It only changes when the new geometry is clear (the contact normal
    points within about 78° of the view direction, either way) or has said so for 3 recomputes in a row.
  - While a pair is undecided (front/back value about 0) it gets no order constraint, as before. A first version kept
    the old order there too; that doubled the order cycles and cost 25% more draw time, so UAT 39 ships this variant.
  - Towers now get an order record like coins.
  - Drawing only; physics untouched.
- Build: `work/cp39/build.py` (pins UAT 38 by hash). Portable patch for any source: `work/cp37-bug/apply_fix.py`.

## Limits
- **Mitts:** coins can still sit under the mitts at the two corners beside the armrests (Wild peak 1–4, Wild 2 peak
  2, Board 3 none). The mitts are locked art hooked over the table corners, and moving coins out from there would
  change the edge physics, so I left it. If Jenny sees it on the phone, it's a small follow-up: the same nudge,
  sideways.
- **Coins in flight** (sky rain, Quake, sweeps) still pass behind the head briefly. That's by design: they come from
  behind the cat.
- **Small residual flicker:** order cycles (three coins each partly in front of the next) are still handled by the old
  clip-mask fallback, so a sliver can still blink there. Wild seed 4 shows 41 such small still-coin blinks of 40–150
  px in 8 s, but no big ones. The next step, if Jenny still sees flicker, is to make cycle handling stable as well.
- **Visible change:** a coin that climbs above the chin line now visibly slides forward off the top of the pile. That
  is the intended "visibly handled"; on the phone it should read as the pile tipping forward.
- **Desktop only.** The desktop canvas library (Node + @napi-rs/canvas) crashes natively (0xC0000409) after a few
  thousand full renders in one process, so long runs render only when they measure. Frame cost is measured in short
  runs (240 frames). None of this is phone FPS; phone look, flicker and FPS need Jenny's UAT.
- Wild/Wild 2 runs cap the prize count and the timer so that a 60 s run stays in play (test only).
