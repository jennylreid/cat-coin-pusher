All four PNGs share a 512 × 640 RGBA canvas at 8 pixels per kitten-local game unit. The draw anchor is (256, 576) in the PNG. To align at the existing anchor after the current kitten transforms, use destination rectangle `(-32, -72, 64, 80)`. The puff art is sized in local units before the game's existing puff and leap scales. The peek uses the ginger run's crown and eyes and crops at local y = −26, the hold's screen-edge line. The cream is a recolour with identical alpha. Bounds are measured at alpha ≥ 16 on the reference stand-ins.

Each comparison shows the stand-in on the left and the painted piece on the right, in two 412 × 915 panels of the same Level 3 frame rendered by the UAT 110 desktop game renderer. An in-memory preview used the existing position, puff/leap scale, tilt and bob. Verified transparency, canvas sizes and a localized pixel difference for each pair. The running PNGs depict one stride phase and cannot reproduce the stand-in's independently moving paws and tail by themselves. The stills cover the two front-facing runs, full puff hang and peek hold. Integration, pose transitions and phone review are pending. Stopped at the art handoff per the ticket.
