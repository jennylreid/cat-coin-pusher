# CP-63 Formal inspection, step 1: prototype report (Claude engineer, Nov 17)

This is a prototype to watch, not a release. I didn't touch `uat\`, `phone\` or any release file. Everything is in
`work\cp63-formal\` and `tickets\CP-63-evidence\`. It's built on UAT 81 (`work\cp81\release.html`, read-only and
checked by hash).

## Side finding (urgent, already sent to the PM)
**UAT 80 and UAT 81 don't start in a real browser.** Chrome shows "Could not start: ReferenceError: Cannot access
'CP62_ART' before initialization".
- Cause: `loadArt()` runs at boot and uses `CP62_ART` in its first lines, but `const CP62_ART={}` is declared further
  down the script.
- UAT 79 doesn't have this. The desktop test harness doesn't catch it.
- A phone WebView should fail the same way. UAT 82 is being built on 81, so it would carry this bug unless it's fixed.
- My prototype build works around it for the watch page only (`build.py`). Nothing in a release was changed.

## The beats (about 4.4 s; the game keeps running at full speed throughout)
1. **0–0.45 s, the monocle.** It drops onto the cat's eye on the left of the screen and bounces a little. There's a
   clink, a glint crosses the glass, and a gold chain hangs over the cheek. The pupil stays visible through the glass.
2. **0.55–1.95 s, the inspection.**
   - "HMM." appears.
   - A soft gold beam runs from the monocle to a lens on the board.
   - The lens visits each chosen piece, from back to front. Each piece gets a glowing gold ring and a small sparkle,
     with a ding that rises in pitch each time.
   - The pupils follow the lens, and the head leans in slightly.
3. **1.95–2.45 s, the cane.** The right big mitt brings a black cane down behind the rearmost lit piece, with a whoosh.
   The cane has a gold collar, a silver tip and a crook in the mitt.
4. **2.45–3.55 s, the sweep.**
   - The cane slides forward across the board's width, with a swipe sound and "SPLENDID.".
   - Each lit piece rides along in front of the cane when the cane reaches it. Pieces are steered inside the tray's
     width so none falls off a side.
   - Pieces pushed along by the lit ones also go over the edge, which gives a little extra spill.
   - Everything falls over the front edge into the normal pickup. The frozen pickup isn't touched.
5. **3.9–4.4 s, the end.** The mitt goes back to rest and the monocle pops off.

**What counts as "most valuable" (prototype default).**
- Up to 5 pieces, ranked in this order: goal prizes, other prizes, the Quake coin, Rain coins by value, then plain
  coins.
- At most 2 of the 5 can be prizes. Without that cap, Level 3 lost all 5 prizes and was won instantly.
- Ties go to the piece nearest the front.
- **The bottom layer stays.** A coin is only chosen if it lies on top of the pile. Prizes always count.

## Limits
- Desktop only. There's no phone, sound or FPS evidence.
- The live watch page wasn't checked in a browser here (the pane was hidden).
- On small screens, the right mitt covers the right edge of the board during the sweep. The "HMM." and "SPLENDID."
  words can cover a lit piece for a moment.
- The wheel's Yawn isn't blocked while an inspection plays. Both use the mitts; this should be guarded when it ships.
