# CP-71 rework: the Yawn pushes coins into the gutter (for the bundled UAT 132, Claude engineer, Nov 20)

Not published by me. Per the PM, it was handed to the CP-92 engineer to go into the bundled UAT 132 (work/cp71-ledge/HANDOFF.md).

Jenny (Nov 20, on UAT 129): "The yawn takes away, it doesn't give to the player... it's reaching toward the side of the
board and pushing the coins into the gutter." "It should probably go after something like a special coin if it's close to
the edge." "It shouldn't eat coins; it should push them into the gutter."

## Phone test (UAT 132)
Shortcut: Settings → Developer → **Wheel lands on YAWN**. Play until no food is on the board first, or use the Endless board.
1. **No food: gutter push.**
   - The cat yawns and side-eyes as before.
   - One mitt goes to the side of the board where the target is, drapes down beside a few coins near that edge, and pushes them sideways over it.
   - They fall into the side gutter and are lost, like any coin over the side. Your coin count doesn't go up from them.
   - No coin shrinks, vanishes or flies off behind the table.
   - If a special coin (rain, quake) is near a side, the mitt goes for that one first.
2. **Food: unchanged.** With food in reach the cat still grabs and eats it ("NOM.").
3. **Unchanged:** the mitt art and faces (no fades), and the pickup when many prizes drop.
```
CP-71 gutter push UAT 132 (device: Pixel / Nothing): 1 pass, 2 pass, 3 pass.
```

## What changed
- `work/cp71-ledge/cp71-gutter.js`, plus one edit in the Yawn's hit, which now keeps the picked coins that are still on the table rather than only top-layer ones.
- **What it picks.** Up to 4 coins on the open part of the table, near one side edge:
  - A special coin near a side comes first, with the coins beside it.
  - Otherwise, the ordinary coins nearest a side where most lie together.
  - Never prizes or toys, and never the front ledge.
- **The push.**
  - The mitt on that side reaches just inside the coins and pushes outward for 0.4 s, then lifts and returns to rest.
  - The coins themselves are driven sideways by the physics, past the low side lip, and fall into the gutter by the game's normal path (`gutterOff`).
- **Removed:** the old flick behind the table, where coins shrank into the distance and were deleted.
- **Unchanged:** food runs, the Yawn's length (about 3.9 s), faces, mitt art and the pickup.

  - Levels 1 and 3 plus a rain coin placed near a side, × 3 seeds × 60/30 fps: 18/18 pass. Every run pushes 4 coins on one side, a special coin first when one is near a side. All go into the gutter via `gutterOff`; nothing is removed inside the Yawn, nothing goes off the front, no errors.
  - Endless board L1, L3, L5, L7, L9, L10 × 2 seeds × 60/30 fps: 24/24 pass. Quake, rain5 and rain10 coins were picked first when near a side.
  - Lab boards run no Yawn, so nothing changes there. The published UAT 131 is the same.
  - Evidence: `CP-71-evidence/gutter/checks71G.json`, `checks71G-endless.json`.
- Frozen pickup (`checks64`) on the UAT 130 trial: all pass.
- CP-71 suite on the UAT 130 trial:
  - Check 2 (food runs identical) passes.
  - Check 4 (seven pickups identical) passes.
  - Checks 1 and 3 fail by design: they assert the old flick behind the table.
- Stills: `CP-71-evidence/gutter/gutter-push-strip-412x915.png` and `-360x640.png` (render harness, Yawn 2.1 to 3.6 s).

  - New check 1: no food, toys only, or a rain coin near a side, on Levels 1 and 3 × 3 seeds × 60/30 fps. The mitt pushes 2-4 coins from one side, all into the gutter, none off the front, nothing vanishing. A rain coin near a side goes first, toys are untouched, and nothing is eaten.

# CP-71 Yawn never eats coins + CP-73 Formal inspection words out of the beam (UAT 90, Claude engineer, Nov 18)

**Published to UAT only: 2026.11.17 UAT 90 / APK7, numeric 20261118000001** (game-only, on UAT 89). Production untouched.

## Phone test (UAT 90)
Update the UAT app (game-only, no new APK). The build should read **2026.11.17 UAT 90**.
Shortcuts: Settings → Developer → open **Engineer tools** → **Wheel lands on YAWN** (the wheel comes up after GO; tap it
or wait), and **Give powers (3 each)** for the Tuxedo's Formal inspection button.
1. **No food in reach: coins knocked back, not eaten (CP-71).** Collect or lose the food first (or play a board with only
   toys in reach), then land the wheel on YAWN. The cat yawns and side-eyes as before, then one mitt comes down on the top
   coins, rummages briefly and flicks 3-4 of them back over the rear edge. They fly back with a spin and drop behind the
   table with real coin clinks, and they're gone. No carrying to the mouth, no "NOM.", no chewing, no eating face. The
   Yawn ends sooner (about 4 s). The flat bottom layer of coins is never touched: no gap.
2. **Food in reach: unchanged.** With food in reach the cat grabs it and eats it ("NOM.", "LOST THE ...!"). Milk is
   drunk and the empty bottle is tossed onto the board. Toys are never taken.
3. **Formal inspection words (CP-73).** Use the Tuxedo's power. "FORMAL INSPECTION!" now shows small, in the band above
   the cat's head (over the hat and goal row), and is gone in half a second, before the monocle's beam starts. "HMM."
   (during the beam) and "SPLENDID." (during the sweep) show the same way. Nothing covers the beam, the lit pieces or the
   cane. Everything else about Formal inspection looks, sounds and behaves as before.
4. **Unchanged:** the Jackpot, and the pickup when many prizes drop at once.
```
CP-71 + CP-73 UAT 90 (device: Pixel / Nothing): 1 pass, 2 pass, 3 pass, 4 pass. Signed off.
```

## CP-71: what changed (on UAT 89)
- Jenny, Nov 17: "CP-71 Yawn: never eats coins. No food in reach = the mitt knocks a few top coins behind the table, no
  chewing. Go, next UAT."
- When there's no food in reach, the mitt picks the same coins as before: up to 4 coins lying on top of the pile, where
  most lie together.
- At its hit it never takes them into the mitt (`cp71Rummage`). It rummages for 0.32 s, then `cp71Knock` flicks the
  coins still lying on top back over the rear edge.
- Each coin is removed from the board and flies back, drawn with its own coin sprite, along a spinning arc to behind the
  table's back edge (z < 190, below the table top), where it's hidden and gone. Each coin plays one of the game's real
  coin clinks as it leaves and a softer one as it drops behind the table.
- The mitt flicks up and returns to rest. There's no carry, no chewing, no "NOM.", no gulp and no eating face. The Yawn
  ends about 1.25 s after the flick (about 4.0 s instead of 4.75 s).
- Food runs are untouched: the same code paths, and identical results in the checks.
- No disappointed look was added (optional in the ticket). The cat's normal face shows after the side-eye.

## CP-73: what changed
- Jenny, Nov 17: "CP-73: move 'FORMAL INSPECTION!' up out of the beam's path, smaller and brief. Add to UAT 90."
- Formal inspection's three words no longer use the big callout over the board. In UAT 89 that callout also queued:
  "HMM." waited for the intro to finish and showed during the beam, as the comparison stills show.
- `cp73Say` shows them in the goal-row band above the cat's head, in the same lettering and colour, at most 28 px tall.
  They keep the same pop sound and buzz.
- Durations: "FORMAL INSPECTION!" 0.5 s (gone before the lens starts at 0.55 s), "HMM." 1.0 s, "SPLENDID." 1.1 s.
- The old callout drew two random numbers each time; `cp73Say` draws the same two, so the game's randomness, and so
  Formal inspection's picks and every later bonus drop, stays exactly as in UAT 89.
- Formal inspection's behaviour, timing, art and sounds are otherwise unchanged.
