# CP-35 Wild board prototype: UAT 35 (November 11, 2026)

**Published 2026.11.11 UAT 35 / APK7**, numeric **20261111224031**, game-only, UAT only.
- **Backup:** `uat/backups/before-CP-35-UAT35-20261111224031`, verified.
- **Cloud:** a fresh download matches the staged file (HTTP 200, same shared item, ETag 71, 51,373,638 bytes).
- **Production:** byte-identical (`phone/` JSON and `CatCoinPusher.apk` hashes checked before and after).
- **Phone receipt:** pending.

**Authorization:** the brief waived art/sound approvals and preview stops. The PM relayed two changes on Nov 11. First, Jenny's goal: collect every prize in a collection; you lose when the time or the coins run out. Second: publish tonight with no more tuning rounds, and add dials for the timer and starting coins.

## How to reach it
A pulsing **WILD!** button in the header, right of the Quake button. While the Wild board is on, it reads **EXIT**.

## What's on the Wild board
- **Goal card:** "Collect all 7 prizes", showing the 7 prizes, then "before the 60 s timer or your coins run out" and a key to the rain coins and bonus drops. Tap **GO!** for a 3-2-1 countdown with beeps and a GO chord. "Back to the game" leaves.
- **During play:**
  - The **timer** sits where the purse countdown usually is.
  - The **collection** is at bottom left: 7 small prize icons, greyed until collected, then ticked.
  - The **Wild bank** starts at 110 coins and is separate from her purse. Front coins pay into it.
- **Collection (7):** Cheese, Shrimp and Tuna (approved art, unchanged), plus four new placeholder prizes drawn in code: Yarn ball, Rubber duck, Toy mouse and Cat bell. All 7 start on the board.
  - Each prize counts once. A repeat pays +5 coins.
  - If a prize she still needs falls off a side, the next bonus drop (within 2 s) brings a replacement, so the goal always stays possible.
- **Rain coins:** big code-drawn coins marked 5, 10 or 25, each with "RAIN".
  - Collecting one gives it the CP-33 moment (rise, hold, ledge bell). Once it is held up, a "COIN RAIN ×N!" callout fires and N free coins fall from above, spread out at about 8–12 a second.
  - A 10 starts near the front (an early wow) and a 5 at the back.
- **Bonus drops**, every 6–9 s from 5 s in: a pulsing "!" ring and a falling whistle mark the spot, then something drops out of the sky with a thud. It's a rain coin (5, 10 or, rarely, 25), a Quake coin, or a prize.
- **Quake coin:** one is on the board and more come as bonus drops. When collected, it flies to the Quake button as in UAT 34, and on this board the Quake is earn-only.
- **Towers:** two coin towers (10 and 12 coins) that topple after a short shove, with a crash and clatter.
- **Turbo pusher:** at 0:25 left the pusher runs twice as fast for 8 s, with a "TURBO PUSHER!" callout and a siren. The pusher keeps its place, so there's no jump.
- **Last 10 s:** a "10 SECONDS!" callout, a big red countdown and a clock tick that gets faster in the last 5 s.
- **Win:** "YOU WIN!", confetti and gold coins, a fanfare with a coin cascade, and the cat does the **Dancing pose** with its drums. The card shows the collection all ticked, time to spare and fun stats, plus **PLAY AGAIN** and **Back to the game**.
- **Lose:** "TIME'S UP!" or "OUT OF COINS!", with a wah-wah stinger. The card shows which prizes she got and which one ran out ("The timer ran out" or "The coins ran out"), plus **PLAY AGAIN** and **Back to the game**.
  - Out of coins means the bank is empty, nothing is raining or dropping in, she has no Quake to use, and nothing has paid at the front for 6 s. While a Quake is waiting the HUD says "Use your Quake!".
- **Developer dials** (Settings → Developer mode → Wild board): **Wild timer** (30–180 s, default 60) and **Wild starting coins** (30–400, default 110). They're saved on the phone, take effect on the next GO or Play again, and have a reset button.
- **Leaving:** EXIT (any time) or "Back to the game" returns to the normal board exactly as it was, with her purse. The purse keeps its normal refill. Nothing on the Wild board touches her save, purse, inventory or collections. Closing the app mid-round reopens on the normal board.

## Difficulty
These are scripted desktop runs on the real runtime: 4 play styles × 3 seeds, 12 runs per setting. The play styles are hold at centre, hold sweeping, hold aimed at the nearest needed prize, and taps every 0.4 s.

| Setting | Quake used? | Wins | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 75 s, 150 coins | no | 11/12 | ~25 s to spare: too easy |
| 60 s, 110 coins | no | 1/12 | 3 lost on coins, 8 on time with 5–6 of 7 |
| 70 s, 130 coins | yes | 12/12 | 22–38 s to spare: too easy (`CP-35-evidence/scripted-runs-70s-130coins.jsonl`) |
| **60 s, 110 coins (shipped)** | yes | 1 evidence run won, 28 s spare | not batch-tested (PM: no more rounds) |

**Pick:** 60 s / 110 coins. Without the Quake it almost always fails. With the Quake, scripted wins finished in 32–48 s, so a first try that doesn't use the Quake well will likely fail, and a good one should win with time to spare. The Quake is the big swing. If it's too easy or too hard on her phone, the two dials adjust it without a new build.

## Sounds (all generated in code)
- **Countdown:** beeps and a GO chord.
- **Bonus drops:** a falling whistle as the marker appears, and a landing thud.
- **Rain:** a whoosh and rising bells when it starts, then a clink per raining coin. The coins also make the game's normal landing sounds.
- **Towers:** a crash plus a coin cascade when one topples.
- **Turbo:** a siren.
- **Last 10 s:** a clock tick, getting faster in the last 5 s.
- **Win and lose:** a win fanfare with a coin shower; a lose wah-wah.
- **Existing:** ledge bell, landing thump, Quake sound, callout bam and the dance drums all play as before.
- **Settings:** the Wild sounds follow the sound setting (off = silent; silent in the background) and the **Pickup volume** dial (0 = silent).

## Limits
- **Placeholder art:** the four new prizes, the rain coins and the goal and end cards are quick code-drawn placeholders.
- **No phone numbers yet:** phone feel, loudness and FPS need her phone.
- **Physics cost:** the Wild board costs about 1.5× Board 3 in physics on desktop. If the phone stutters during a 25-coin rain, the first fix is fewer start rows or a slower rain.
- **Difficulty:** the shipped 60 s / 110-coin setting wasn't batch-tested with the Quake in use (see above).
- **Win screen:** the result card covers part of the board, but the dancing cat stays visible above it.
- **Developer mode:** with Developer mode on, the Wild board still uses earn-only Quake; the normal boards are unchanged.
- **Not committed:** nothing is committed or pushed yet. Close-out after sign-off.

## What to try (sound on)
1. Accept the update. The footer shows **2026.11.11 UAT 35**.
2. Tap **WILD!** in the header, read the card, then tap **GO!**
3. Play a round. Push the purple 10 off early and watch the coin rain. Watch for "!" rings, and use the Quake when you earn one.

5. Win, or get close: collect all 7, and the cat dances.
