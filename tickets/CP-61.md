## 4. Backup mitigations
- **Gold 5s, earlier: used.** With the slow-down gone, the bot's own drops went up 15 a round. Without a backup, its
  peaks rose a little (up to 141 holding, 148 tapping). So CP-48's merge (five resting coins become one gold 5, with
  a glint and soft chime) now starts at 100 plain coins instead of 130. Nothing slows.
- **Physics sleeping: works, but not during play.** A calm board sleeps completely (53/53 coins). The game only lets
  coins sleep 3.5 s after the last drop, so while you keep dropping, nothing sleeps (0% at every peak). Letting coins
  sleep during play would change how the pile responds, so I didn't change it. It's an option if phones still stutter.
