## 1. Dance break fix (my bug since UAT 75)
- **The bug:** in UAT 75 I put a comment on the Dance hit line, in front of `D.bank[kind]++;`. Everything after `//` is
  ignored, so an on-beat hit banked nothing. The counter stayed at 0, and the payoff paid 0. The CP-57 engineer found it.
- **The fix:** the bank line runs again, with the same line as `work/cp57-money/build-dancefix.py`. Half as many rain
  coins (Rain 5 only) is kept.
- **New check 8:** 16 beats tapped on time bank 16, and the payoff pours them (2 seeds). UAT 77 banked 0.
- **How it slipped through:**
  - My checks only proved each edit was applied once (check 0). They never played a Dance break.
  - I didn't rerun CP-59's Dance runs (`checks73.cjs`) on my build.
  - My bot never dances.
  - The edit swapped part of a long line and added a comment, without looking at what followed on that line.
- **Same mistake anywhere else:** I scanned every CP-61 comment in UAT 78. No other comment has code after it on the
  same line.

## 2. The Yawn (wheel slice and Tuxedo's YAWN power)
- **Why UAT 77 could knock off nothing:**
  - Only plain coins could go. Prizes, rain coins and power coins on top were ignored.
  - Some of the coins it chose sat beside CP-21's side walls (depth 380-431), where nothing can go over the side.
  - Pushing stopped at 2.05 s, so coins stopped by a prize stayed on.
  - The few coins that did go left under the paws and were easy to miss.
  - On desktop, UAT 77 still knocked off 3-6 coins, so I couldn't reproduce an exact zero.
- **Now:**
  - Any piece lying on top of the pile can go: coins, rain coins, gold 5s, power coins (Quake, note, cat coins), prizes.
  - Only pieces where the sides are open are chosen, the highest first.
  - Each Yawn takes a handful, with a prize counting as 2. So it's a couple of prizes, or a prize and a few coins, or
    5-6 coins.
  - Each piece is pushed until it leaves the table.
  - Nothing else is pushed. Same animation, sound and timing.
  - A goal prize knocked off comes back through the usual lost-prize drop.
- **Check, mid-round Level 3 boards (Tuxedo), 4 seeds, at 60 and 30 fps, both Yawns:**
  - 5-8 pieces off every time, a mix of coins, silver, prizes and the music note;
  - the bottom layer kept its coins, and at most 3 more gaps than with no Yawn (top coins sliding off drag it a
    little; UAT 74's sweep left 10-11 gaps);
  - early thin board: 4 off;
  - full board: 8 off, bottom layer kept, drops still pay.

## 3. No merging, gold 5s only when coins are dropped in
- **Coins on the board never turn into a gold 5,** at any count. Check: 141 plain coins for 10 s gave 0 merges (UAT 77: 2).
- **Rain, pours and payouts past 100 plain coins on the board** are dropped as one gold 5 per five coins from the start.
  They fall from the sky as gold 5s and all land. In UAT 77 they fell as plain coins and 4 in 5 vanished on landing
  (check: a RAIN 25 dropped 5 gold 5s, all landed; UAT 77 dropped 25, and 18 vanished).
- **The player's own drops are always plain coins:** one tap is one coin, so the rule is unchanged.

## 4. Backup mitigations
- **Gold 5s, earlier: used.** With the slow-down gone, the bot's own drops went up 15 a round. Without a backup, its
  peaks rose a little (up to 141 holding, 148 tapping). So CP-48's merge (five resting coins become one gold 5, with
  a glint and soft chime) now starts at 100 plain coins instead of 130. Nothing slows.
- **Physics sleeping: works, but not during play.** A calm board sleeps completely (53/53 coins). The game only lets
  coins sleep 3.5 s after the last drop, so while you keep dropping, nothing sleeps (0% at every peak). Letting coins
  sleep during play would change how the pile responds, so I didn't change it. It's an option if phones still stutter.
