## 2. Music: "Kawaii bubbly pop" on every board
- **Track 4 from the audition** (144 BPM, 20 bars, a 33.3 s loop) replaces the UAT 38 loop on Boards 1–3, Wild and Wild 2.
  - It's generated in the game from the audition's own code (copied unchanged into `kawaii41.js`), not an embedded WAV.
  - At start-up that code fills a note list (2,250 notes, no sound). The music then plays the list a little ahead on the audio clock, round and round.
- **The dance-break hand-off is unchanged:** the background stops as the count-in starts, and comes back from the top of the loop after the break.
- **Music volume:** 0–200%, default **100% = the audition's level** (the audition's mix gain is baked into each note). The dial is saved under a new key, so every phone starts at 100%; her pusher setting is kept.
- **No limiter:** even 200% can't clip, so I dropped the one I had planned for the old track. The old-track kick trim is gone too.
- **Coin sounds:** unchanged.
- **Levels** (`uat41/levels.json`, rendered from the game's own sound calls; dBFS at the phone's output; "phone band" = RMS above 250 Hz):

  | | RMS | Peak | Phone band RMS |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | UAT 40 old track, 50% (old default) | −34.7 | −10.7 | 0.0123 |
  | **UAT 41 kawaii pop, 100% (default)** | **−28.6** | −8.8 | **0.025** (audition: 0.0225) |
  | UAT 41 kawaii pop, 200% (max) | −23.2 | −4.9 | 0.047 |

  - **Against the old track:** the default is about 6 dB louder overall, and twice as loud above 250 Hz.
  - **Against the coins:** a coin landing's hit is still about 6 dB above the music's RMS, so the music sits under the action.
  - **No clipping:** at 200%, a coin landing on a music peak sums to 0.95 of full scale.

## 3. Pusher beat and Turbo
- **Every stroke now has a beat:** a mechanical "chunk" as the pusher sets off (forward louder, back softer), a light ratchet while it travels and a small knock where it stops. It plays twice per 3.2 s cycle on every board, on the existing Pusher sound dial (default 50%).
- **Turbo:** twice the strokes (8 in 6.4 s instead of 4), about 4 semitones higher and about 3 dB louder. It drops back when Turbo ends.
- **The callout** reads **"TURBO PUSHER! 2× SPEED"** (on both Wild boards). Frame: `uat41/turbo-callout.png`.
- **Levels:** RMS −44.7 / peak −19.1 dBFS (UAT 40's swish: −53.7 / −30.9), well under the coins.
- **Limit:** most of the new energy is the low thump (about 96 Hz), which a phone speaker barely plays. Above 250 Hz it's no louder than before (0.0013 against 0.0016). On the phone, the click and ratchet carry the beat. If it's too faint, the Pusher sound dial goes to 100%. A brighter knock is a small follow-up.

# CP-38 fix: the dance-break freeze, UAT 40 (November 12, 2026)

**Published 2026.11.12 UAT 40 / APK7**, numeric **20261112152400**, game-only, UAT only. It's UAT 39 (CP-37) plus this fix, and nothing else.
- **Backup:** `uat/backups/before-UAT40-20261112152400` (UAT 39), verified.
- **Cloud:** a fresh download matches the staged file (HTTP 200, same shared item, ETag 76, 51,458,814 bytes).
- **Production:** byte-identical (hashes checked before and after).
- **Phone receipt:** pending.
- **Source:** `work/cp40/build.py` applies `work/cp40/fix_crash.py` to published UAT 39 (checked against its manifest). Evidence: `CP-38-evidence/uat40/`.

**Authorization:** the PM approved the fix plan and said to publish straight away (Nov 12).

## What Jenny saw
On UAT 38, the game froze when a Wild 2 dance break ended: the music stopped and nothing moved again. Settings still opened.
- **Her phone log:** frames stop about 10.5 s after "dance start", which is the count-in plus 16 beats. That's the moment the payoff starts.
- **Wild 2's own log line** for the payoff ("finale v2") never appears, so the code stopped just before it.

## Cause
- **The bug:** a Wild 2 sound passed a number where the audio destination goes. `sfx.wild2('total')` called `_mt(196,t,.5,'sawtooth',vol,392)`, where the 6th argument is the node to connect to, so it ran `connect(392)`. Chromium throws "Failed to execute 'connect' on 'AudioNode'".
- **When it hit:** it runs as a Wild 2 break ends with coins banked: right away if imperfect, after PERFECT if perfect. The visitor sound after a Wild 2 win had the same mistake twice.
- **Why it froze the game:** the game's frame loop has no try/catch, so one exception stops it for good. The page itself stays alive, which is why Settings worked.
- **Why my desktop checks missed it:** it only happens with sound on, and my UAT 38 checks ran muted or with a lenient fake audio.
- **Reproduced:**
  - in real Chromium (the UAT 38 build served locally, sound on): the exact error at the payoff;
  - in the desktop runtime with a strict fake audio: every Wild 2 break throws, and Wild 1 breaks don't.

## Fix (three changes, nothing else)
1. The three calls pass the master output as the destination, as intended.
2. The two sound helpers fall back to the master output if they're ever given something that isn't an audio node.
3. **Frame-loop guard:** if any frame throws, the error is written to the performance log as an `error` event (with the message and line) and the next frame runs. A future bug can't freeze the game silently, and her log will say what happened.

## Validation (`CP-38-evidence/uat40/checks.json`: 18 pass, 0 fail; desktop, sound on, strict fake audio)
- **Boards 1–3:** identical to UAT 39 after 25 s of play.
- **Controls:** UAT 39 still throws at the end of a Wild 2 break and on the visitor sound (proving the checks catch it).
- **UAT 40:** each of these breaks ends, with no exception and the game still running:
  - Wild 2 imperfect;
  - Wild 2 perfect landing on each wheel slice (+10, +15, RAIN 25, QUAKE, +20, PRIZE, YAWN);
  - Wild perfect and Wild imperfect.

  Live audio nodes settle back to 15–22 after each break, with no build-up. A Wild 2 win shows the visitor card with its sound.
- **Frame guard:** a frame that throws is logged as an `error` event, and the next frame runs.
- **Stress** (on a copy of UAT 38 with this fix): repeated breaks at a phone-like 12 FPS with 3 physics steps a frame and about 110 extra coins. No exceptions, live audio nodes flat at about 14, and the pour, fx, confetti and pickup lists drain to 0.

## Not in this build
**Wild 2 frame rate** (11–17 FPS on her phone, physics at 45–73 ms a frame): measured, and the plan is with the PM. CCD only accounts for about 14% of the physics time; the coin count is the main cost. It goes in a later build.

## What to try

2. With sound on, play Wild 2 to a dance break and let it end: imperfect once, and perfect once if you can. The payoff, the pour and the wheel play, and the game carries on.

## What's in Wild 2 (everything Wild has, plus)
1. **Music the whole time (on every board).**
   - **The background track:** generated in code. A bouncy 112 BPM loop in F major, 8 bars (F–Dm–Bb–C, F–Am–Bb–C): a soft kick, a rim click, a shaker, a bass, a soft chord pad, a marimba tune, and a little "meow" slide at the end of each loop.
   - **Where it plays:** I added it to **Boards 1–3 and Wild too**, at the same default volume (50%), because it schedules cleanly and doesn't touch play. It stays on under the goal card, countdown, Settings and the bag.
   - **The dance break takes over:** the background fades out (0.25 s) as the count-in starts, the dance music plays, and the background comes back from the top of its loop once the break, the pour and any wheel are done.
   - **Other quiet moments:** it also stops for the win dances (the board-clear drums and the Wild win), the Wild end screen, the collection dance and the background.
   - **Pusher sound:** a soft mechanical slide on every stroke on every board. It's a gentle swish forward with a little clunk where the pusher stops, and a quieter swish back. It plays twice per 3.2 s cycle and stops when a board ends.
   - Both follow the **sound setting**. **Developer dials:** Music volume and Pusher sound (0–100%, default 50%). Both are saved and in the performance log (`soundTuning`).
2. **A clear dance payoff.**
   - **During the dance:** the beat lane is unchanged. Under it, a **big gold counter grows** with each banked beat ("+1", "+2"…, the number getting bigger), with "COINS BANKED" and the streak. Banked coins fly into it, and banked rain coins sit above it.
   - **When the music stops:** the roar and shake, then **"+13 COINS!"** big in the middle (and "+ 2 rain coins" if any) for about a second.
   - **The pour:** then the coins pour from **above the top of the screen, falling past the cat** onto the table, spread over about 1 s, with clinks as they land. Rain coins land mid-table to be pushed off.
   - The **confetti/sprinkle burst is gone** on Wild 2.
3. **Perfect dance = the wheel.**
   - Hit all 16 beats: **"PERFECT!"** (green, "every beat!", a rising fanfare). Then the total and the pour, then **"PERFECT SPIN!"**: a prize wheel spins over the cat for about 3.4 s, ticking past each slice, and lands with its result.
   - **Slices (chance):**

     | Slice | Chance |

     | +10 coins (two slices) | 45% |
     | +15 | 18% |
     | +20 | 14% |
     | **RAIN 25** | 5% |
     | **QUAKE** coin | 5% |
     | **PRIZE**, a collection prize she still needs | 4% |
     | **YAWN** | 8% |

     Coins pour from above. RAIN 25, QUAKE and PRIZE drop near the front.
   - **YAWN:** "UH OH… YAWN", then "YAAAWN…" and a long, sliding yawn sound. The cat's paws (the approved mitt art, unchanged) lift, **slide forward onto the table** and **sweep outward** ("SWOOSH!"). Coins in their path fly off the sides, about 15–18 in the tests. Then the paws go back.
   - The round timer stays paused until the wheel (or yawn) is done.
4. **Coin rain from higher.** On Wild 2, rain-coin rain and the dance pour start above the top of the screen and fall past the cat. They become real coins just above the table and land to be pushed off. Wild 1's rain is unchanged.
5. **A taste of the Porch.**
   - **The card:** after a Wild 2 win (once the cat has danced about 2.4 s), a **"A VISITOR CAME!"** card shows over the cat. It has a placeholder cat face drawn in code, "Orange cat loved your tuna!", the visitor's personality line ("· new visitor!" the first time), and "Visitors seen: N of 4" with the four faces, greyed until seen. Tap to close; then the win card works as before.
   - **Which visitor:** the mapping from GAMEPLAY_DESIGN.md (tuna → Orange cat, cheese → Ragdoll, shrimp → Siamese, toy mouse → Tortoiseshell). A cat not seen yet comes first; among those, the one whose treat she collected first that round.
   - **Visitors seen** is kept on the phone (`catpusher.uat.visitors.v1`, UAT only). There's no visitor on Wild 1.
6. **Developer** (Settings → Developer mode → "Wild 2 and sound", under Dance break):
   - Music volume and Pusher sound.
   - **Wild 2: spin the wheel now**, and **Wheel lands on …** for each slice. These open Wild 2 if needed and spin right after GO.
   - **Wild 2: show a visitor now**: a forced win with a visitor.
