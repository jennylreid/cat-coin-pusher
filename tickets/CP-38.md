# CP-38 fix: the dance-break freeze, UAT 40 (November 12, 2026)

**Published 2026.11.12 UAT 40 / APK7**, numeric **20261112152400**, game-only, UAT only. It's UAT 39 (CP-37) plus this fix, and nothing else.
- **Backup:** `uat/backups/before-UAT40-20261112152400` (UAT 39), verified.
- **Cloud:** a fresh download matches the staged file (HTTP 200, same shared item, ETag 76, 51,458,814 bytes).
- **Production:** byte-identical (hashes checked before and after).
- **Phone receipt:** pending.
- **Source:** `work/cp40/build.py` applies `work/cp40/fix_crash.py` to published UAT 39 (checked against its manifest). Evidence: `CP-38-evidence/uat40/`.

**Authorization:** the PM approved the fix plan and said to publish straight away (Nov 12).

## What Jenny saw
On UAT 38, the game froze when a Wild 2 dance break ended: the music stopped and nothing moved again. Settings still opened.
- **Her phone log:** frames stop about 10.5 s after "dance start", which is the count-in plus 16 beats. That's the moment the payoff starts.
- **Wild 2's own log line** for the payoff ("finale v2") never appears, so the code stopped just before it.

## Cause
- **The bug:** a Wild 2 sound passed a number where the audio destination goes. `sfx.wild2('total')` called `_mt(196,t,.5,'sawtooth',vol,392)`, where the 6th argument is the node to connect to, so it ran `connect(392)`. Chromium throws "Failed to execute 'connect' on 'AudioNode'".
- **When it hit:** it runs as a Wild 2 break ends with coins banked: right away if imperfect, after PERFECT if perfect. The visitor sound after a Wild 2 win had the same mistake twice.
- **Why it froze the game:** the game's frame loop has no try/catch, so one exception stops it for good. The page itself stays alive, which is why Settings worked.
- **Why my desktop checks missed it:** it only happens with sound on, and my UAT 38 checks ran muted or with a lenient fake audio.
- **Reproduced:**
  - in real Chromium (the UAT 38 build served locally, sound on): the exact error at the payoff;
  - in the desktop runtime with a strict fake audio: every Wild 2 break throws, and Wild 1 breaks don't.

## Fix (three changes, nothing else)
1. The three calls pass the master output as the destination, as intended.
2. The two sound helpers fall back to the master output if they're ever given something that isn't an audio node.
3. **Frame-loop guard:** if any frame throws, the error is written to the performance log as an `error` event (with the message and line) and the next frame runs. A future bug can't freeze the game silently, and her log will say what happened.

## Validation (`CP-38-evidence/uat40/checks.json`: 18 pass, 0 fail; desktop, sound on, strict fake audio)
- **Boards 1–3:** identical to UAT 39 after 25 s of play.
- **Controls:** UAT 39 still throws at the end of a Wild 2 break and on the visitor sound (proving the checks catch it).
- **UAT 40:** each of these breaks ends, with no exception and the game still running:
  - Wild 2 imperfect;
  - Wild 2 perfect landing on each wheel slice (+10, +15, RAIN 25, QUAKE, +20, PRIZE, YAWN);
  - Wild perfect and Wild imperfect.

  Live audio nodes settle back to 15–22 after each break, with no build-up. A Wild 2 win shows the visitor card with its sound.
- **Frame guard:** a frame that throws is logged as an `error` event, and the next frame runs.
- **Stress** (on a copy of UAT 38 with this fix): repeated breaks at a phone-like 12 FPS with 3 physics steps a frame and about 110 extra coins. No exceptions, live audio nodes flat at about 14, and the pour, fx, confetti and pickup lists drain to 0.

## Not in this build
**Wild 2 frame rate** (11–17 FPS on her phone, physics at 45–73 ms a frame): measured, and the plan is with the PM. CCD only accounts for about 14% of the physics time; the coin count is the main cost. It goes in a later build.
