## What was built (all 14 brief items, in one UAT build)
Source `work/cp49/progress49.js`, inlined by `work/cp49/build.py` into pinned UAT 48. Save: `catpusher.uat.progress.v1` (new section); Developer dials: `catpusher.uat.progressdev.v1`.
1. **Home** is the first screen: the current cat lounges (breathes, blinks, its eyes wander), taps get the sweet face, hearts, a meow and a purr. Big doors: PLAY (the board), Kitchen, Porch, Album, Shop. A level badge with an XP bar, coins and Catnip, a settings gear. A pink paw print on a door when something waits (a cat on the porch or paw prints, a recipe ready to cook or new, a new photo, a new board, an affordable Shop item the recipe needs). Doors creak and swing open. Music plays on Home, Kitchen, Album and Shop.
2. **Cat boards** (map section 4): Tuxedo = Wild 2, Orange = Treat toss, Ragdoll = Cozy, Siamese = Laser chase, Tortoiseshell = Tower wall. A new save has only the Tuxedo's board. With two or more boards, PLAY opens a board picker: before level 5 only the newest board plays, the others show a lock and "Level 5"; unopened boards are dark with "?". The MODES picker is under Developer (and a MODES chip on Home when Developer is on).
3. **Goals and stars:** each round's collect list is 5 prizes (levels 1–2), 6 (3–4) or 7 (5+), drawn fresh each round from the board's pool, weighted 6× toward what the current recipe still needs and 2× toward the board's own prizes. The round's star prize is a needed one when there is one. 1★ = goal met, 2★ = met with **20 s or more left**, 3★ = 2★ plus a perfect side game (perfect dance, treat toss or pet; laser chase with no misses and 3+ hits). The goal card's visitor line is now the stars line. The end card shows the stars, XP, coins to the jar, Catnip and any level-up.
4. **Exclusive prizes:** each board drops its 2 "only here" prizes (bow tie and music notes; tuna and yarn; cheese and cushion; shrimp and bell; toy mouse and duck) plus 5 shared ones (fish biscuit, milk, egg, feather, sardine). Every recipe needs another cat's "only here" prize; until that board is open, it's in the Shop.
5. **Kitchen:** recipes open by level: Tuna dish (Orange, level 2), Cheese plate (Ragdoll, 3), Shrimp skewers (Siamese, 4), Fish pie (Tortie, 5), Formal supper (Tuxedo, 6). Cooking: drag or tap each ingredient into the pot (plop, splash), then STIR: draw circles in the pot (or tap it) for 2½ turns while a ring fills, then a big DING!, steam and the dish. The recipe is used up when cooked (you hold one copy); a recipe coin on a board wins it back. Ingredients are used only at the ding, so leaving mid-cook loses nothing.
6. **Porch:** garden with clouds, swaying flowers, birds that chirp. 2 food spots. Food set out → after a short wait it's eaten: "Someone ate the tuna 🐾" (shown below the cat's chin during a round, under the top bar on the Porch, above the doors on Home), a bell if you're on the Porch. The cat sits by the bowl for a hidden window, in real time, also while the game is closed. Tap the camera (or the cat): shutter, white flash, the photo flies to the Album. Missed: paw prints, the food's gone; tap them to tidy up. The visiting-cat card is gone from every board.
7. **Album:** a prize page (15 prizes, dim until first won, with counts) and a page per cat with 3 poses (calm, sweet, wild: one per photo). Flip with the arrows or a swipe (page-flip sound). A full page stamps COMPLETE: a cat page gives 3 Catnip, the prize page 5.
8. **Shop:** SELL PRIZES (tap the price: coins fly into the jar, clink, the jar wobbles) and BUY INGREDIENTS (only what no opened board drops; what the recipe needs is listed first and highlighted; the till rings). The jar fills with your coins.
9. **Cat coins:** after a cat's first photo, its coin can drop as a bonus drop on any board (with a whistle and "ORANGE'S CAT COIN!"). Pushing it off the front opens that cat's board with the big moment: rays, the cat pops up, fanfare, confetti, "ORANGE'S BOARD IS OPEN!"; the round timer holds while it plays. Off a side, it comes again another round.
10. **Caps:** 9 of each prize (Developer dial). Extras turn into coins at once at the sell price, with a clink and a "+n".
11. **Placeholder cats:** recoloured **copies** of the Tuxedo pictures (head and its faces, mitts), made in code at load, per cat, with a name tag; the Tuxedo's own art is untouched and comes back when a board ends. Porch cats: the recoloured head on a simple code-drawn sitting body. Copies are built one picture per frame while you're on Home, so boards open without a hitch.
12. **Dance: one move per dance.** Each dance break and each win dance uses one of the Dancing pose's 4 moves, never the same as last time (remembered across rounds).
13. **Feel:** every screen has sound (doors, plops, stir swish, bubbles, ding, shutter, bell, birds, page flips, stamp, clinks, till, fanfare, lock thunk), motion and something to tap; text is the backup. All new sounds follow the sound setting and the Pickup volume dial; music follows the Music dial.
14. **Developer → Gameplay map (CP-45):** Visit window (real, 20 s, 30 s, 1, 2, 5, 10 min), XP speed (1×, 2×, 5×, 10×), Item cap (1–30), Give all cat coins (opens every board), Drop a cat coin in the next round, Cat visits now, Give 3 of every prize + 300 coins, Reset progression, and MODES: Wild 2 and each mode, plus "Old boards 1–3".

**Save upgrade:** on first open, UAT 48's purse coins and treats carry over; anything over the cap becomes coins once (checked: 500 coins + 12 tuna → 9 tuna + 545 coins).

## Tuning
- **XP:** a round 10 + 4 per prize + 15 per star; cooking 15; a photo 25; a board opening 30. Level L→L+1 needs 50 + 30×(L−1) XP (50, 80, 110, 140 …). A first win reaches level 2; level 5 is about 6–8 rounds plus the chain.
- **Level unlocks:** 2 Kitchen, Porch, Shop and the Tuna dish; 3 Cheese plate; 4 Shrimp skewers; 5 Fish pie and board switching; 6 Formal supper.
- **Coins:** every coin pushed off the front during a round also goes to the jar (gold 5s count 5), plus 10 per star. Desktop: about 35–60 per round. Sell: 8 (shared prize) / 15 ("only here"). Buy: 30 / 60. So a missing "only here" ingredient is about a round of coins.
- **Caps:** 9 per prize; extras → sell price each.
- **Porch:** eaten 45–90 s after it's set out; the cat stays 20 min to 2 h (random, hidden). With the Developer dial on: eaten within 8 s, stays the dial's time.
- **Catnip:** 1 for a 3★ round, 3 for a full cat page, 5 for the full prize page.
- **Drops:** cat coin 30% per bonus drop once a round is 8 s in, at most one a round; recipe coin 15% per bonus drop after 12 s, at most one a round, only when a needed recipe is used up.
