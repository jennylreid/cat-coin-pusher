## UAT 53: Quake coin on the default pickup, no "Wild", the dance on the beat (Jenny, Nov 14, via the PM)
**Published: 2026.11.14 UAT 53 / APK7 (20261114220000). Waiting for Jenny's phone test.** UAT 53 is UAT 52 plus three changes.

1. **The Quake coin is collected like everything else.** In UAT 52 every prize and special coin used the default pickup (the "Pop"), except the Quake coin. It had its own older pickup: it went to the middle of the screen, grew to 70% of the screen in front of a grey pinstriped box, held for 0.7 s, then flew to the Quake button. Now it uses the same pickup as the prizes, starting from where it fell:
   - the bell as it goes over;
   - a pop up near that spot, to the same size (120 px) with the same soft shadow;
   - the same 0.2 s rise, 0.25 s hold and 0.45 s leave, in the same line.
   
   It still ends at the Quake button, where its chime plays and the count goes up (its own sound and its gameplay). Removed: the centring, the 70% zoom and the box.
   - The default pickup itself is unchanged (frozen). Every prize and coin that already used it traces frame for frame the same as UAT 52. Seven prizes dropping at once give the same line, bells, thumps and overlap as UAT 52.
   - A Quake coin in a pile-up now takes a normal turn (UAT 52's held the line for an extra 0.2 s).
   - The old box style can't come back, even from an old saved setting. Its switch in Developer is gone, along with the dials only it used.
   - Frames, UAT 52 above and UAT 53 below: `CP-45-evidence/uat53/pickup/quake-before-after.png`, plus `cheese-before-after.png` and `tuna-before-after.png` (unchanged, as expected).
2. **No "Wild" in anything a player sees.**
   - The Tuxedo's board is "Tuxedo" for now (the name waits on Jenny): TUXEDO on the goal card and in the board readout, and Tuxedo in the board picker, the Album page and Developer's MODES list.
   - The other boards keep their mode names.
   - What still says Wild (the old boards' WILD!/WILD 2 buttons, and the Developer tuning) is only reachable with Developer on.
3. **Dance break: the cat dances on the tap beat.** UAT 52's moves ran 3 cycles per bar of 4 beats and faded in and out every bar, so they drifted 50–270 ms off the beat. Now every move lands on each beat of the judge's clock, the same instant the beat sound plays and the ring pulses:
   - paws slam down and the body bobs to its lowest point, alternating sides each beat;
   - move 0's eyes reach each side on the beat.
   
   The learned tap offset stays on the taps. It is the player's touch delay, so tapping when the cat lands is judged on the beat. The count-in and the finale are unchanged.
   - **How I checked the sync:** the cat's position was sampled every frame (60 fps clock) for all 4 moves, with sound on and off. For each beat I found the frame where it lands and compared it with the judge's beat time. All moves land within 2–5 ms of every beat, under one frame, sound on or off; UAT 52 was 50–270 ms off. Results: `uat53/dance/beat-sync.json`; frames: `uat53/dance/move1-sheet.png`.

## Tuning
- **XP:** a round 10 + 4 per prize + 15 per star; cooking 15; a photo 25; a board opening 30. Level L→L+1 needs 50 + 30×(L−1) XP (50, 80, 110, 140 …). A first win reaches level 2; level 5 is about 6–8 rounds plus the chain.
- **Level unlocks:** 2 Kitchen, Porch, Shop and the Tuna dish; 3 Cheese plate; 4 Shrimp skewers; 5 Fish pie and board switching; 6 Formal supper.
- **Coins:** every coin pushed off the front during a round also goes to the jar (gold 5s count 5), plus 10 per star. Desktop: about 35–60 per round. Sell: 8 (shared prize) / 15 ("only here"). Buy: 30 / 60. So a missing "only here" ingredient is about a round of coins.
- **Caps:** 9 per prize; extras → sell price each.
- **Porch:** eaten 45–90 s after it's set out; the cat stays 20 min to 2 h (random, hidden). With the Developer dial on: eaten within 8 s, stays the dial's time.
- **Catnip:** 1 for a 3★ round, 3 for a full cat page, 5 for the full prize page.
- **Drops:** cat coin 30% per bonus drop once a round is 8 s in, at most one a round; recipe coin 15% per bonus drop after 12 s, at most one a round, only when a needed recipe is used up.
