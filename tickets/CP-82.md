## What changed
Jenny, Nov 18: "For all the powers, the rule should be the words appear before the action is taken, because signs like meow
and tortitude, in order to see them clearly, you want the words out of the way. Hug, it probably doesn't matter as much."
Go: "we definitely want to do CP82."
- Copied from Formal inspection (CP-73) and Zoomies (CP-72). On the tap, the power's big name shows alone: same lettering,
  place, pop, tilt and fade as before, for 1.2 s. The action starts 1.25 s after the tap.
- The action is UAT 100's own code, unchanged (`build.py` checks it byte for byte): the same sounds, shake, sound rings and
  5 s of 2× reach (Sonic meow), and the same flames, flash, shove and confetti (Tortitude). It is only started later.
- "2× REACH" is no longer drawn over the rings. Other callouts wait until the power has ended.
- If the round ends during the title, the power ends with no action (as a round end already cuts a running power).
- Hug is unchanged; Jenny said it matters less. It still has its callout and "NO SIDE FALLS". That is the one power that
  doesn't follow the rule yet.
- DEVELOPER.md "Powers" now has the rule: every power shows its name first, then the action with no words over it.
- Source: `work/cp101/build.py` + `cp82-titles.js` (+ `cp72-zfix.js` for the Zoomies fix).
