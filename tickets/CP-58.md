## The beats (about 4.75 s; the old Yawn was 2.8 s)
1. **0–1.4 s, the yawn and stretch:** head tips back, eyes squeeze shut, mouth opens wide, the existing "YAAAWN…" text
   and yawn sound; both big mitts stretch up and out beside the head and shiver at the top.
2. **1.5 s, the side-eye:** eyes pop open and the game's own pupils slide up and away, with a little tick sound.
3. **1.4–2.25 s, the "arm around someone":** one mitt comes down wide and drapes slowly onto the board.
4. **2.45 s, the grab:** it snaps onto the prize nearest the front (a goal prize first; the Paw save's whoosh and
   swipe sounds). **2.8 s:** the other mitt grabs a second prize. The cat keeps looking away while it grabs.
5. **3.1–4.2 s, eating:** both prizes are carried to the mouth; four chomps (the Treat toss catch sound), crumbs,
   "NOM.", eyes closed happily, then a gulp (plop).
6. **4.3 s, the steal is undone:** each stolen goal prize comes back through the game's own bonus drop (marker,
   whistle, drops from the sky), with "IT'LL DROP BACK!" / "THEY'LL DROP BACK!". Checked: Level 1 shrimp and
   cheese, Level 3 box and egg all came back; no duplicates (while a prize is in the mitt the bonus-drop clock doesn't
   count it missing).

**No coin gaps:** the new Yawn never touches coins. It only lifts prizes, and prizes sit on top of the coin bed.
It works for both places the Yawn is used: the wheel's Yawn slice and the Tuxedo's YAWN power. The power ends at
2.8 s as before while the Yawn plays out; the power can't be reused until the Yawn has finished.

## Effort to ship it properly
- **Code: medium (about a day).** The prototype is ~120 lines on top of UAT 74 (`work\cp58-yawn\cp58-yawn.js`):
  timing table, mitt paths, face overlay, grab/eat, replacement through the bonus drop. To ship: tidy, add checks
  (no coin moved by the Yawn, stolen goal prize always comes back, no duplicates, round end/pause/Home during the
  Yawn, the frozen pickup unchanged), small-screen shots, phone UAT. Works on any board with prizes.
- **Art: the main cost.** A yawning-mouth face (or mouth overlay) for the Tuxedo, one piece on the head canvas, is
  needed for this to look finished. Optional: a chewing mouth. Squeezed eyes could stay code-drawn.
- **Sound: small.** A real chomp/munch and a gulp (from the sound library, approved in-game per CP-46/54).
- **Risk: low to medium.** It never touches coins, so the old gap problem goes away. Risks: the frozen pickup is not
  touched but a stolen prize leaves the board, so the Level goal count must be checked carefully; the round clock
  and the pause/Home paths during the 4.75 s; the big mitts reaching the front row on small screens.
