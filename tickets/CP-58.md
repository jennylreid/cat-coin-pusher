**Published to UAT only: 2026.11.16 UAT 82 / APK7, numeric 20261116235953** (game-only, on UAT 81). Production untouched.
The coin count with no prize in reach is being tuned in UAT 83 (see check 2).

## Phone test (UAT 82)
Update the UAT app (game-only, no new APK). The build should read **2026.11.16 UAT 82**.
**UAT 80 and 81 didn't start** ("Could not start: … CP62_ART …"). UAT 82 fixes that, so first check that the game opens
and a round starts.
Quickest way to see the Yawn: in Settings (Developer on), under "Wild 2 and sound", tap **Wheel lands on YAWN**. A round
starts and the wheel comes up after GO; tap it to spin (or wait), and it lands on YAWN.
1. **The game starts.** The title screen shows, and a round plays. The bow ties and other CP-62 prizes look as before.
2. **An eaten prize is lost.** The cat yawns (round Runway face), side-eyes and grabs the prize nearest the front (goal
   prizes first), and eats it with "NOM.". At the gulp you should see "LOST THE CHEESE!" (or whichever prize), hear the
   lost sound, and see the goal icon shake, the same as a prize knocked over the side.
   There is no "IT'LL DROP BACK!" and nothing drops straight back.
3. **It comes back the normal way.** If there's time left, a lost goal prize returns later through the usual bonus drop
   ("BONUS DROP INCOMING!"), a few seconds after the wheel is gone. You can still finish the level with it.
4. **No prize in reach: coins.** If no prize is in reach (e.g. after collecting them all), the mitt grabs coins from the
   top of the pile and eats them (in UAT 82 usually just 1; UAT 83 makes it a few). No gap is left in the coins lying
   flat on the table.
5. **Tuxedo's YAWN power** and the **pickup** (many prizes at once) are unchanged.

## What changed (on UAT 81; CP-62 kept)
- **Eaten = lost.** At the gulp, each eaten prize gets the same handling as a prize that goes over the side: the lost
  sound, the goal icon shake, "LOST THE …!", the round's lost count, and the star's 12 s return. Left out: the side-rail
  flash and the prize tumbling over the edge, because it was eaten, not knocked off. While it is still in the mitt it
  doesn't count as missing yet.
- **No drop-back.** The immediate drop-back and "IT'LL DROP BACK!" are removed. A lost goal prize comes back only
  through the normal lost-prize bonus drop, with its usual timing. That clock waits while the wheel is up, so the prize
  returns about 2-6 s after the wheel closes, as long as there's time left in the round.
- **Never nothing.** With no prize in reach, the mitt grabs up to 4 coins lying on top of the pile, nearest the front
  and near each other (the pile builds up toward the back, so it reaches back to just in front of the pusher). It eats
  them. The bottom layer is never touched.
- **Boot fix (UAT 80/81 regression):** CP-62's `CP62_ART` / `CP62_COLOURS` are now declared above the boot line.
  `loadArt()` runs at boot and fills CP62_ART, which hit "Cannot access 'CP62_ART' before initialization" in a real
  browser. The desktop harness strips the boot line, so it never saw it. Nothing else about CP-62 changes.
- **Edge cases:**
  - If the prize falls into the tray before the mitt reaches it, the mitt grabs air (as in UAT 79).
  - On a completely flat board with no prize, no coin lies on top, so the cat only yawns. In real play the wheel
    comes after a Dance break pours coins, so this shouldn't happen.

# CP-58 Yawn, step 1: prototype report (Claude engineer, Nov 16)

A prototype to look at, not a release. Nothing in `uat\`, `phone\` or any release file was touched. Everything is in
`work\cp58-yawn\` and `tickets\CP-58-evidence\`.

## How to watch it
- **Video (quickest):** `tickets\CP-58-evidence\yawn-level1.mp4` (Level 1: cheese and shrimp) and `yawn-level3.mp4`
  (Level 3: five prizes). Also `yawn-level1.gif`. Rendered from the real game at 412x915. No sound in the videos.
- **Live, in the real game on the PC (with sound):** start the review server (the `coinpusher-review` entry in
  `.claude\launch.json`, or `python -m http.server 8795` in the CoinPusher folder), then open
  `http://127.0.0.1:8795/work/cp58-yawn/watch.html` in Chrome. The round starts by itself (allow ~10 s to load);
  tap the board once for sound, then press **Play the Yawn**. Buttons for a new Level 1 / Level 3 round and to add a
  cheese, sardine or shrimp.
- **Key beats (412x915):** `tickets\CP-58-evidence\L1-1-before.png` to `L1-12-after.png`, plus `L3-*.png`.

## The beats (about 4.75 s; the old Yawn was 2.8 s)
1. **0–1.4 s, the yawn and stretch:** head tips back, eyes squeeze shut, mouth opens wide, the existing "YAAAWN…" text
   and yawn sound; both big mitts stretch up and out beside the head and shiver at the top.
2. **1.5 s, the side-eye:** eyes pop open and the game's own pupils slide up and away, with a little tick sound.
3. **1.4–2.25 s, the "arm around someone":** one mitt comes down wide and drapes slowly onto the board.
4. **2.45 s, the grab:** it snaps onto the prize nearest the front (a goal prize first; the Paw save's whoosh and
   swipe sounds). **2.8 s:** the other mitt grabs a second prize. The cat keeps looking away while it grabs.
5. **3.1–4.2 s, eating:** both prizes are carried to the mouth; four chomps (the Treat toss catch sound), crumbs,
   "NOM.", eyes closed happily, then a gulp (plop).
6. **4.3 s, the steal is undone:** each stolen goal prize comes back through the game's own bonus drop (marker,
   whistle, drops from the sky), with "IT'LL DROP BACK!" / "THEY'LL DROP BACK!". Checked: Level 1 shrimp and
   cheese, Level 3 box and egg all came back; no duplicates (while a prize is in the mitt the bonus-drop clock doesn't
   count it missing).

**No coin gaps:** the new Yawn never touches coins. It only lifts prizes, and prizes sit on top of the coin bed.
It works for both places the Yawn is used: the wheel's Yawn slice and the Tuxedo's YAWN power. The power ends at
2.8 s as before while the Yawn plays out; the power can't be reused until the Yawn has finished.
