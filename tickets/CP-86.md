## UAT 116: E final, a longer hold and a quick return with no fade (Nov 19)
**Published to UAT only: 2026.11.19 UAT 116 / APK7, numeric 20261119000027** (game-only, on UAT 115; the CP-88 hotfix

Jenny, Nov 19: "I agree with E, but I want an adjustment. I want the [paws] to stay above the head a little bit longer with
no fade back to home, just quickly back to home." Then: "The fade is in the midst as they move down from above the head back
down to base... I prefer it if that split second is used to keep the hands or the mitts above the head a little bit longer."

**What the fade was.** The mitts were never faded: every mitt drawing in the meow is fully opaque, with the same art and the
same size (measured in real Chrome and checked on every frame). The cause was the drawing order:
- While raised, the mitts are drawn in front of the head; at rest they belong behind it (the idle mitts are drawn before the
  head).
- Back at rest they overlap the cheeks. So on the frame the face switched back, the top half of each mitt vanished behind the
  cheeks while it was still moving. On the phone that reads as the mitts fading on the way home.

**The fix.**
- The mitts hold above the head until 4.65 s, longer than UAT 115's 4.3 s.
- They come down quickly in front of the face to just under the cheeks by 4.8 s (0.15 s). There no part of them overlaps
  the head, so the change to behind the head happens there and shows nothing.
- They tuck up into rest behind the head by 4.9 s (0.1 s), so the face still switches back on a still cat.
- E is the only behaviour. The "Sonic meow mitts" choice is removed from Settings (not hidden); a choice saved earlier on the
  phone, or none, gives E.

## What it does
Jenny, Nov 18: "for the Siamese cat, we can use the yawn face with Sonic Meow, don't you think?" Go: "CP-86 Sonic meow: the
Siamese shows its open-mouth yawn face during the meow. Try it in the next UAT."
- Reuses the existing CP-75 Siamese yawn face (`img/art/cp75-yawn-face-siamese.webp`, already in the game). Nothing was
  redrawn and no art was added.
- It is drawn the way the wheel's Yawn draws it: over the normal face, on the head's own canvas.
- Timing: off during the CP-82 title; fades in over the first 0.15 s of the action; held while the meows and rings play;
  fades out with the rings over the last 0.4 s.
- Drawing only: one small block, `work/cp86-meow-face/cp86-meow.js`.

## Add-on (Nov 18): the meow shakes the cat; the mitts move
Jenny: "let the meow shake the whole cat and let the paws move around. Choose some action for the paws so the cat just doesn't
look totally passive with a giant open maw."
- A fast shiver of head and mitts that peaks on each meow. Sonic meow meows at 0, 1.25, 2.5 and 3.75 s of its action.
- On each meow the head lunges forward a little (6 units), and both mitts push forward (40) and inward (22), turning in.
  The lunge builds over 0.08 s and settles over about 0.9 s.
- Between meows the mitts knead the board in turn (12).
- Never upward, so it doesn't read as the Yawn's stretch.
- The approved head and mitts are only moved and turned, never redrawn or resized: the head through `petHeadXf`, the mitts
  through `pawMotion`, as the other poses do.
- All of it scales with the face's fade, so it builds in and settles back to rest. Drawing only; the mitts' colliders don't
  move.
- Eyes: the squint through the first three meows; on the last meow (3.75 s into the action) the eyes switch straight to
  open (no fade) and stay open. Open eyes = the Siamese's own eyes and pupils from its normal head painting over the squint (`CP86.openAt`).
