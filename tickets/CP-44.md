# CP-44 Tower wall

## Prototype (Nov 12, engineer; desktop runs on a copy of the UAT 45 work-in-progress; nothing published)

To be built on UAT 47 and published as UAT 48 once the channel shows UAT 47 and `tickets/CP-43.md` has its report.

### What it is (`work/cp48-bug/tower48.js`, full build `work/cp48-bug/proto48.html`)
- **A mode of its own:** "TOWER WALL" in UAT 45's MODES picker ("Topple the towers, win the prizes on top"), built on Wild 2. Wild 1, Wild 2 and the other modes are unchanged.
  - Its goal card has Wild 2's six-line layout: towers, Paw save, happy meter and dance, the star prize and visitor, rain coins, bonus drops.
- **The wall:** five coin towers side by side across the board, 58 units apart, at depth 470. Heights are 13, 18, 23, 18 and 13 coins. Coins under the towers are removed, and rain or Quake coins move out of the way.
- **Prizes on top:** five of the round's seven prizes sit one on each tower. The round's star prize (UAT 43) goes on the middle, tallest tower; the other two stay on the floor as on Wild 2.
  - The prizes are placed after the board settles: placed before, the star slid off the tall tower while the board settled, and the rain coin knocked the middle tower over.
- **Sideways topple:** a wall tower tips after a short shove (3–7 units, as on Wild). When it starts to tip, its top also gets a push toward the nearer side (the middle tower picks a side at random), so it leans out over the edge more than forward.
  - Prizes on top often go over a side. With CP-24 they fall there, unless Paw save (CP-40) catches them.
- **Developer → Tower wall:** "Sideways topple" dial (default 1.1, 0 = as on Wild) and "Tower wall: start a round now".

### Desktop rounds
90 s each, hold-to-drop with the aim sweeping wide; seeds 1–6; the round timer is held so all 90 s play. Results are for the five top prizes.

| | Won off the front | Lost off a side | Star prize won |
|---|---|---|---|
| No Paw save taps | 9 of 30 (1–2 per round) | 20 of 30 | 5 of 6 rounds |
| Tapping every glow (3 saves) | 8 of 30 | 22 of 30 | 5 of 6 rounds |

- **Hard but possible:** 1–2 of the 5 top prizes are won per round. The outer towers' prizes usually go off a side 10–16 s in. Paw save glows 3–11 times a round.
- **Saves don't change the count yet:** a saved prize lands about 80 units inside the edge (CP-40's flick) and often goes over again as the wall keeps toppling. A stronger flick toward the middle in this mode would make a save count for more. That's a Phase 2 tuning point; CP-40's behaviour on Wild 2 stays as published.
