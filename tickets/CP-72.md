## What changed from the approved prototype (v2)
- **No hold at the edge** (Jenny, Nov 18). The front-lip prize hold is removed.
- **New rule, so the kittens keep to 1 prize without a hold.**
  - What I found: with the hold gone, the release checks (mid-round, after 12 s of real play) showed 3–9 prizes going
    over per use on Levels 2 and 4, against 0–1 without the power.
  - The cause: the probes showed it wasn't the fast pusher, which alone tipped 0–1. It was the coins the kittens pushed,
    ramming other prizes off the front.
  - Now a piece only joins a kitten's shove if no other prize lies in its path to the front edge, and it's let go if one
    moves into its way. Nothing is held and nothing looks different; on crowded boards the kittens just push fewer
    coins.
  - A softer version (coins stopping short of a prize) leaked up to 3 extra prizes, so I didn't use it.
- **The release file.** `index.html` is the only changed runtime file: UAT 90 plus the CP-72 block and the build name.

# CP-72 Zoomies, step 1: prototype report (Claude engineer, Nov 17)

This is a prototype to watch, not a release. I didn't touch `uat\`, `phone\` or any release file. Everything is in
`work\cp72-zoomies\` and `tickets\CP-72-evidence\`. It's built on UAT 89 (`work\cp89\release.html`, read-only, checked
by hash).

## Why "nothing happens" today (UAT 89, `release.html`, the "powers" block: `pgpUse`/`pgpTick`/`pgpKitten`)
- **The kittens run sideways and barely touch anything.** Seven kittens run left or right across random rows. Each
  coin a kitten passes gets one small nudge: mostly sideways and up, only a little forward. Prizes, Rain coins and
  Quake coins are never touched. Nothing gets near the front edge, so nothing is paid. In the "today" video, the board
  before and after is the same.
- **The double speed is easy to miss.** The pusher's period does halve (3.2 s to 1.6 s). But the slide sound only
  switches to its faster "turbo" voice for the wheel's TURBO. During Zoomies it plays the normal sound, just more often.
  There's nothing moving on the pusher to show the speed. Only "2× PUSHER" tells you.
- **Why the art fails on the Orange cat.** The kittens are tiny side-on shapes, about half a coin long. They're painted
  in the Orange cat's own fur colour (`PG_CAT.orange.fur`, #ee8a2c), with no outline. On the Orange board they cross a
  carpet of gold coins that's almost the same colour, so they vanish (`today-412x915.png`).

## What the prototype does (about 7 s)
1. **0 s.** "ZOOMIES!", the turbo rev and a meow. The pusher runs at double speed for 7 s. While it does:
   - the slide plays the turbo version (higher, brighter, a bit louder);
   - gold speed streaks show on the pusher's top, and its front edge glows on each forward stroke;
   - "2× PUSHER" shows as before.
2. **0.45 s, 1.9 s and 3.35 s: a pair of kittens each time (6 in all).**
   - Each kitten pops out from under the cat's mitts at the back of the board (a rustle) and hops down.
   - It then runs straight toward you, with little dust puffs behind it and a meow on landing.
   - Each kitten is aimed at the best piece left: prizes first, then Quake, Rain and coins lying on top.
3. **The shove.** What's in a kitten's lane rides just in front of it, with a clink as each piece joins, and is steered
   inside the tray's width.
   - Up to 4 pieces per kitten, and at most 2 prizes per use.
   - Only prizes and pieces lying on top are shoved. The bottom layer of coins stays, so no gap opens that drops can't
     reach.
4. **The front edge.** The kitten skids with its eyes squeezed shut. Its pieces go over into the tray: the normal,
   frozen pickup (prize pop-up, coins landing, COIN RAIN for Rain coins). The kitten leaps off toward you, growing and
   fading, with a whoosh.
5. **Fair play.** While Zoomies runs, any prize (or carried piece) that drifts to a side edge is eased back toward the
   middle, the same guard Formal inspection uses. I added it after one run where the double-speed pusher knocked an
   untouched box off a side.

## Checks (`work\cp72-zoomies\check.cjs`, `CP-72-evidence\check.json`)
I played the power 15 times through the real button: Levels 1–5 × 3 seeds, at 60 fps, each against the same window
with no power.
- **Every use worked.** 64 pieces were carried by kittens and all 64 went over the front edge: 30 prizes (never more
  than 2 per use), plus Quake, Rain and coins on top.
- **Nothing a kitten carried and no prize fell off a side.** 2 plain coins fell off a side with the power, against
  0 without it (Levels 2 and 4). That comes from the double-speed pusher.
- **The pusher:** 1.6 s per stroke during the power and 3.2 s after, every run.
- **The lock:** a second tap does nothing while it plays.
- No leftover state afterwards and no frame errors.
- **Level 1:** the kittens take the cheese and the shrimp, which finishes Level 1 straight away.
