## What changes (behaviour list)
1. **Developer, in this order:**
   - The Endless board buttons, moved up from the bottom of Engineer tools.
   - **Start now:**
     - Start Dance break now, at the top.
     - Side games: Treat toss, Laser chase, Pet the cat, Mouse pounce.
     - Powers: Formal inspection (Tuxedo), Zoomies (Orange), Hug (Ragdoll), Sonic meow (Siamese), Tortitude (Tortie).
     - The wheel buttons, Start a Jackpot now, Cat visits now.
   - Basics.
2. **Engineer tools, grouped:** Give, Dance and music, Start a board round, Previews, Boards and tuning, Logs, Reset.
3. **Each side game and power button** starts it at once:
   - **Where:** on the board you're on, the endless board included. From Home it uses the current cat's own board.
   - **Cost:** no coin is used, and your own power stock is unchanged.
   - **If something else is running:** if another side game or power is running, it starts right after that one ends.
   - **Any power on any board:** a power from another cat runs its own effect on the board you're on.
   - **Fish:** the fish coin is how Treat toss starts, so "Fish" is the Treat toss button.
4. **Start Dance break now** uses the current cat's board and its own dance (Tuxedo, Orange, Ragdoll, endless board). It used
   to open the Tuxedo's board from Home. The Siamese and the Tortie show "No dance for this cat yet".
5. **Music A/B/C row (Developer only):** during the Orange's and the Ragdoll's Dance breaks, a "Music A B C" row sits under the
   lane.
   - A tap switches the music from the next beat and saves it, the same choice as in Engineer tools.
   - It's never a dance tap.
   - With Developer off there's no row.
6. **Removed** (hidden; their code and IDs stay):
   - The level buttons: Open every level, Pass the next level, Back to Level 1, Start on level −/+, Reset levels.
   - Give all cats, Give all cat coins, and +1,000 coins. The prize gift stays.
   - The duplicates.
   - The old per-game start buttons, including the broken Treat toss one. It switched to the Orange's board rather than
     starting a game.
7. **Unchanged:** the levels themselves, every value and slider, the gameplay, and the frozen pickup.

   buttons, no "+1,000 coins", no old "Start treat toss now".

3. Tap each Power button. That power plays on the board you're on, and your own power count doesn't change.
4. From Home, tap Start Dance break now. You get the current cat's board and its own dance. On the Siamese you get a short
