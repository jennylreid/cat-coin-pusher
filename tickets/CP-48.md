### 1. The main-screen freeze: what I found
- **Nothing stays stuck after leaving a heavy board.** I flooded Level 6 as the Ragdoll with Hug to 131 coins, then left two ways: the end card's "Main screen" and the in-round "Board" button. After both, every PLAY-screen button and PLAY itself worked.
  - No overlay was left up and no tap handler was lost.
  - The board doesn't run behind the PLAY screen: physics already stops off the board (UAT 55).
- **One stall on the way out.** The in-round "Board" button resets the board twice (it reloads the old board, then the level). That is 360 physics steps in one go: about 0.3 s on desktop, likely 1–3 s on the phone, with taps ignored meanwhile. "Main screen" on the end card resets once (about 0.1 s). With the board now near 100 coins instead of 130, the stall is shorter. I didn't change the exit path (not in scope); it can be a later fix.
- **Cause, as far as the logs go:** CP-47's finding still holds. About 130 coins pin the phone's WebView (physics 45–60 ms a frame), and then the page itself stops. The new logging (section 3) will show what the screen was doing when it stops.

### 2. The coin cap (about 100)
**Rule as built:**
- The coin budget from UAT 43 now starts at 100 plain coins on the board (was 130). Above that, twice a second, five resting coins near the back turn into one gold 5, worth the same 5 coins, with its glint and chime. Coin rain, pours and sky coins past 100 land as one gold 5 in five.
- **"Board full" (backstop):** at 120 coin pieces (plain coins plus gold 5s), a tap or hold drops nothing. The coins stay in the bank, and a short "Board full" shows twice: at the front of the board (like "Easy taps") and in the board's message line. Drops come back as soon as coins fall off.
- Never capped or changed: prizes, rain coins (5/10/25), the Quake, powers and the pickup (DEVELOPER.md "Pickup (frozen)").

**Why not a hard stop at 100 (as the brief first said).** I built and measured it, and it broke play:
- It made Levels 3, 6, 7 and 10 unwinnable for the bot: 0/3 each, with drops blocked 20–90 times a round. The PM was told mid-ticket.
- The reason: a board at about 100 coins jams. With no new coins the pusher moved nothing off the front in 30 s, so the player could only wait out the clock. UAT 62 jams the same way at 130.
- The gold-5 merge holds the board at the same size without blocking anyone.

**Results:**
- A full-speed hold-flood now peaks at 102–108 plain coins (UAT 62: 129–133). Physics per tick on desktop:

  | When | UAT 63 | UAT 62 |
  |---|---|---|
  | During the flood | about 9 ms | 11–18 ms |
  | After the flood | 10–13 ms | 16 ms |

- Normal play (bot, all ten levels) peaked at 82–118 pieces and was never blocked.
- In Jenny's freeze replay the gold 5s keep the board under 120, so "Board full" only shows on an extreme or long flood.

### 3. Logging added
Saved where the existing UAT log goes (`catpusher.uat.diagnostics.v1` and the connected log file, `uat/CatCoin-UAT-performance.json`):
- The last **200 events** (was 40), also kept from the previous session.
- **Saved at once on the first error**, with a `first-error-state` event (screen, coins, bodies, holding, the last 3 taps). Window errors and unhandled rejections are caught too.
- **A local save every 5 s during a round, even while holding.** Before, saves waited for the hold to end, so the last seconds of a flood were lost. The next launch reports that session as `previous`.
- **`long-frame`** for frames over 100 ms: ms, physics ms, draw ms, steps, coins, bodies and the screen name (at most one every 2 s, with a count of the skipped ones). A gap over 5 s logs as `frame-gap`.
- **The last 10 taps** (`taps`): time, x, y, what took the tap (for example `play`, `side:kitchen`, `board-button`, `end:main`, `power`, `board`), the screen before and after, and the coin count.
- **Screen changes** (for example `board:play > board:end (102 coins)`, `main+cats`), `round-end` with the level, cat and peak coins, `peakCoins` for the session, and `board-full` events.
- Not in this ticket (needs an APK): native `onRenderProcessGone` and a heartbeat watchdog, the only way to tell a hang from a renderer crash. **Later ticket.**
