Today: what the game plays now

When your coin lands on the board, the game picks one of 5 sounds at random (never the same twice in a row): the recorded landing coin-land.mp3 or one of 4 drops on the pile made in code (CP-46). The title screen plays only the code-made "steel" clink.

How these play

Holding plays a coin every 0.40 s for 6 s; fast taps play one every 0.25–0.30 s for 6 s. Like the game, a landing within 0.09 s of the last is skipped, and each coin picks a different variant from the one before. Candidates A–D also vary pitch by ±6% each time (E doesn't: it plays exactly as UAT 59 did), the same range the game's code-made clink uses; the game doesn't vary a landing's volume, so neither does this page. A–D are matched in loudness to today's 4 code-made drops, the level CP-46 set against the music (loudest 100 ms above 250 Hz; E stays at the game's own level, so it is louder; ).
