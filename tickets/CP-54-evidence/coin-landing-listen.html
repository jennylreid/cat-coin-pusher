Today: what the game plays now

When your coin lands on the board, the game picks one of 5 sounds at random (never the same twice in a row): the recorded landing coin-land.mp3 or one of 4 drops on the pile made in code (CP-46). The title screen plays only the code-made "steel" clink.

A. A coin onto a pile of change: lower and chunkier

A single coin dropped onto coins: a dull clack and a short clatter of the coins around it. Three takes in rotation.

Source: voicebosch-single-coin-170397.mp3 (in the project folder), cut at 19.09 s, 23.98 s and 12.97 s.

B. A little pour of change

Busier: like a few coins landing in a heap at once. Slices of the coin-spill recording, three in rotation.

Source: freesound_community-coin-spill-105867.mp3 (in the project folder; also the game's coin-spill.mp3), cut at 1.66 s, 2.39 s and 0.90 s.

C. A coin onto change: brighter rattle

A single coin with a higher, lighter rattle as it settles among other coins. Three takes in rotation.

Source: voicebosch-single-coin-170397.mp3, cut at 3.71 s, 9.92 s and 5.63 s.

D. Made in code: change hitting change

A dry clack and a quick scatter of tiny jingles, all over in about 0.2 s. No low "plop" and no long pure ring. Each one is different.

Source: Original, made in code with the same sound calls the game uses; no recording, no licence needed.

E. Before Nov 14: the recording every time

Every landing plays the recorded coin landing, exactly as UAT 59 and the Oct 31 production build played it: the same file, volume 0.25, cut at 0.84 s, no pitch change, 0.09 s gap, at most 3 at once. Not level-matched: this is its real level, about 13 dB above the code-made drops. Its title pile-up is the same steel clink as today; the title has played that since it was added.

Source: coin-land.mp3 = dragon-studio-dropping-a-coin-478359.mp3 (project folder), the same file in UAT 59 and UAT 70 (MD5 checked).

F. A, with a soft pour underneath

Each landing is one of A’s coin-on-pile takes with a short, quieter slice of the coin pour (B’s recording) under it, starting just after the strike, so the pile seems to shift and rustle. The take and the pour slice both change each time; the pour lasts 0.2 s so fast taps stay clear. Matched in loudness to A.

Source: voicebosch-single-coin-170397.mp3 (A’s three takes) + freesound_community-coin-spill-105867.mp3, four 0.2 s slices at 1.10, 1.45, 1.85 and 2.20 s, 9 dB under the take.

How these play

Holding plays a coin every 0.40 s for 6 s; fast taps play one every 0.25–0.30 s for 6 s. Like the game, a landing within 0.09 s of the last is skipped, and each coin picks a different variant from the one before. Candidates A–D also vary pitch by ±6% each time (E doesn't: it plays exactly as UAT 59 did), the same range the game's code-made clink uses; the game doesn't vary a landing's volume, so neither does this page. A–D are matched in loudness to today's 4 code-made drops, the level CP-46 set against the music (loudest 100 ms above 250 Hz; E stays at the game's own level, so it is louder; ).
