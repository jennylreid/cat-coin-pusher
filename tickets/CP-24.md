# CP-24 Prizes tip off the sides

## Phase 1 findings (Nov 12, engineer; desktop runs, prototype on a copy of UAT 42, nothing published)

Planned for UAT 44 together with CP-40 Paw save (report in `tickets/CP-40.md`). Phase 2 applies this on UAT 43 once the PM says it is published.

### Why a fish hanging off the side doesn't fall
1. **A hidden lip props it up and tips it back.** CP-21 added a low invisible lip just outside each open side edge, 0.9 coin radius out and 4 units high. Without it, more than half the coins went off the sides and no board could be won. The lip's top is 4 units above the table, so anything hanging over the edge rests on a slope that leans back toward the table.
   - In the probe, a prize placed with its centre 0–20 units past the edge slid back to 10–15 units inside.
   - At 25 past the edge it sat on the lip for good: the fish is drawn well over half off the table and never falls ([frames](CP-24-evidence/cp24-tuna-overhang-before-after.png), top row).
2. **The game's own contact fix-up ignores collision filters.** Its extra push-apart pass (solidCoinContacts) pushes prizes out of every fixed shape, the lip included. Excluding prizes from the lip in the physics alone didn't help: the prize hung in mid-air at lip height.
3. **Balanced past the edge, a prize goes to sleep.** A prize resting on an overhanging coin stops moving, the physics engine puts it to sleep, and it never tips.
4. **Wild and Wild 2 hit this most.** Their bonus prizes land anywhere on the board, so they often reach the sides. Boards 1–3 prizes start mid-table and, in the test play, always went out the front.

### Prototype (`work/cp44-bug/fix24.js`, applied by `apply24.py`)
- **Lip:** prizes pass through it; coins still use it. This uses a Rapier collision group, and the game's own contact pass now applies the same group rule.
- **Tip rule:** a prize whose centre of mass is past an open side edge stays awake and gets a gentle outward push (0.4 m/s) until it falls.
  - Each prize's art is centred on its physics shape, so its centre of mass is the drawn centre. "Centre past the edge" means more than half the drawn prize is over the edge, Jenny's "more than halfway off".
  - Tuna: centre 10 units inside the edge stays; at the edge or past it, it tips and falls.
- **Prizes on coin piles:** the tip rule covers a prize perched on overhanging coins, which used to sleep there. A separate outward slide near the edge is available as a dial (pileSlide) but showed no measurable effect, so it is off.
- **Coins:** the same rule applies to coins (coinTip 0.4). Coins hanging more than half off now fall; coins with their centre on the table are held by the lip as before. That gives the "fair amount" of extra side spill without bringing back the CP-21 flood.
- **Dials (constant CP24):** lip distance, lip height, tipAssist, coinTip, pileSlide. Phase 2 can put the useful ones under Developer if the PM wants.

### Evidence (desktop)
**Fish over the side edge.** On Board 3, the tuna is placed on the bare edge with its centre 25 units past it ([frames](CP-24-evidence/cp24-tuna-overhang-before-after.png)).
- UAT 42: still hanging at 0.1 s, 0.6 s and 1.5 s.
- Prototype: gone by 0.6 s.

**Spill per 60 s round.** A drop every 0.22 s with the aim sweeping ±60 across the drop line; seeds 1–5; board ends switched off so every run plays the full 60 s. Each cell is the total over 5 rounds.
- "Hang" counts the seconds a prize sat alive with its centre past a side edge, which is what Jenny saw.

- **Coin share:** side coins rise from 22% to 27% of all coins off the table. Single rounds vary a lot: Board 2's side coins ran from 4 to 42 per round. Treat per-board differences under about 30% as noise.
- **Wide aim** (±105, 2 rounds each, sides): Board 1 28 → 29, Board 3 16 → 20, Wild 2 25 → 40. Prizes still left mostly by the front.
- **Balance, Boards 1–3** (as CP-37: 3 seeds each, drop every 0.22/0.3/0.4 s, until the board ends):
  - UAT 42: 9/9 wins, 687 coins used.
  - Prototype: 9/9 wins, 685 coins used. No run lost a required prize off a side.
- **Variants tried:**
  - Lip moved out to 1.0 radius: side coins went down on some boards and up on others, with no clear gain.
  - Lip 2 units high: mixed, up to +100% on Wild 2.
  - The coin tip rule is the most even increase, so it's the pick.
- **Data:** `tickets/CP-24-evidence/runs/` (spill/, wide/, balance/).

### Limits and open points
- Desktop only. How it feels and how often Jenny sees prizes go off the side need her phone test.
- On Boards 1–3 a required prize lost off a side ends the round ("PRIZE LOST"). The rule is now honest, so that can happen a little more when prizes drift sideways. It never happened in the balance runs.
- Phase 2 re-measures on UAT 43. UAT 43 adds gold "5" coins and a coin budget on the Wild boards, which the coin rule will include.
