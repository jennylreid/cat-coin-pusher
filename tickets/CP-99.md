# CP-99 Treat toss meter out of the way: report (engineer, Nov 19)

**Status:** **published as UAT 125** (2026.11.19 UAT 125 / APK7, numeric 20261119000036, game-only; package 47,709,789
bytes; cloud copy verified, same shared item). Built on UAT 124 in `work/cp125`.
- Pre-publish set, all pass: checks99 5/5, 64 (frozen pickup) 10/10, 92e 13/13, 93g 9/9, 97s 12/12.
- The full regression is held for the next combined run.
- checks99 confirms:
  - the meter is beneath the board on a tall screen and at the top on a short one;
  - the toss plays exactly as on UAT 124 on both: treats, catches, streak, coins and every piece, every frame.

Jenny: "the measuring stick that displays how much you've captured. It's actually in the way of the capturing. So I'd like
it to be moved elsewhere on the board so that it's not in the middle of the game."

## Where the treats go (measured)
During a whole toss, the treats fly between y ≈ 200 and y ≈ 770 (game units, 400 wide), across nearly the full width, at both
412x915 and 360x640. The meter sat at y 468, in the middle of that range.

There's no room below the table on a 360x640 phone, because the table reaches the bottom edge.

## Where it goes (Jenny: "I think it'd be better beneath the board.")
- **Beneath the board**, where there's room: in the gap under the table's front edge and above the bottom corner text (the
  mode name, "Collect all 5!" and the paws). The gap is about 35 units tall and the bar 40, so the bar is scaled down evenly
  (about 0.85x) to fit. This is the 412x915 and 393x873 case.
- **Fallback, just under the top bar** (centre y 140), above the highest treat, where the gap is under 30 units. Short screens
  such as 360x640 have no gap at all, because the table reaches the bottom edge, and 412x846 has only 13. There it covers the
  level name and goal row while the toss runs.
- It's the same bar with the same contents: treats caught, the dots for treats left, and the streak or "TAP THE TREATS".
- Unchanged: the TREAT TOSS! title, the end words, the treats, the timing, where caught treats fly, and the frozen pickup.
