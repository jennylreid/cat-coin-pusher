## How to reach it
Tap the **MODES** pill in the header, under WILD! and WILD 2 (normal board only). The GAME MODES picker opens below the cat's chin. Tap **TREAT TOSS**.

## The Treat toss mode
- **Board:** Wild 2's board as it is in UAT 44:
  - the goal card with TAP TO START, the 60 s timer and the coin budget;
  - collect all 7 prizes, with the star prize;
  - rain coins, bonus drops, towers, Turbo, the music, the Paw save and tipping prizes;
  - the win and lose cards and the visitor.
- **Its own goal card** (teal), with these lines:
  - "Push the fish coin off and the cat tosses treats!"
  - "Tap treats in the air to catch them"
  - "Catch every one for a RAIN 10 coin"
  - The star prize / visitor line
  - Rain coins and bonus drops
- **Left out (they're Wild 2's feature):** the Dance break, the happy meter, the music-note coin and the wheel.
- **Who runs it:** the Tuxedo, for now. No new cat art.

## Treat toss (the side game)
- **Trigger:** a fish coin, a teal coin with an orange fish marked "TREATS". It arrives as one of the bonus drops, once a round (the 3rd to 7th drop). A second one can come if the first is lost off a side. Push it off: it pops like any rain coin, and the toss starts about 0.3 s later.
- **The toss** (about 8.5 s, plus a 0.9 s intro and a 2.1 s end):
  - "TREAT TOSS!" appears below the chin and the round timer pauses (⏸ beside it). Turbo and the bonus-drop clock pause too. The board's physics keeps running.
  - The cat flips 16 treats up from behind the board in arcs. They use the approved sprites as they are, drawn 72 units wide with a soft light glow, so they read apart from the same prizes lying on the board.
  - Treats launch on the music's eighth-note grid. Arcs vary in height, angle, spin and speed.
  - **Ramp:** a few treats at first, then more and faster, with pairs late on.
  - Every tap is a catch attempt. No coins drop during the toss, but EXIT and Settings still work.
- **Feedback:**
  - **Catch:** a snap and chomp, plus a pop whose pitch climbs with the streak, and a short buzz.
    - A ring and sparks; "+1", or "NEW!" for a prize the goal needs, or "YUM!" / "PURR-FECT!" every 5 in a streak.
    - The treat flies into the bag, the bag bounces, and the landing thump plays.
  - **Miss (a tap on air):** UAT 43's Dance break miss, a red ✕ that pops plus the muted "bonk", and a soft whiff.
  - **A treat that falls back:** a smaller red ✕ where it fell and a soft plop.
  - **Strip below the chin:** catch count, treats left, and the streak.
- **End:**
  - "+N TREATS!" with a line like "2 for the goal · 9 to the pantry", plus confetti and a chime.
  - **Catch every one:** "PERFECT!", and a RAIN 10 coin drops from the sky.
- **Goal and pantry:**
  - Each toss brings at most **2** prizes the goal still needs; caught ones tick off the collection at the end. Every other treat is one already collected, a spare that goes to the **pantry**. The pantry is saved on the phone, ready for the recipe idea.
  - **Why the cap:** without it, a sharp player collected all 7 prizes in one toss, and the toss won the round on its own.
- **Rules:**
  - A win waits until the toss is finished.
  - A dance break never runs during a toss (and in this mode there are none).
  - EXIT ends the toss cleanly.
  - Nothing lasting sits on the cat's face: the strip, words, cards and picker are below the chin, and only the flying treats pass over it.

## Pattern for the next modes (CP-42 Laser chase, CP-43 Pet the cat)
- **Modes** (`modes45.js`): a mode is a set of settings on top of Wild 2, added with `registerWildMode({id, name, blurb, colors, panel, features:{...}, cardLines, icon, cardIcon})`. It then appears in the MODES picker, gets its own goal card (name, colours, lines) and shows its name in the HUD.
- **Side games** are self-contained pieces that switch on with `wildFeature('<name>')`. Treat toss is `toss45.js` plus `toss-sounds.js`, and `mode-treats45.js` registers the mode. Wild 1 and Wild 2 have no mode and keep the Dance break.
- **Hooks into existing code:** five one-line hooks, each returning Wild 2's own value when no mode is on:
  - the happy meter fill
  - music-note coin scheduling
  - the happy-meter HUD
  - the HUD name
  - the goal card's name, panel and lines
- **Not done: the Kitchen recipe** (e.g. 3 tuna, 3 carrots, 3 eggs). It needs a goal that counts repeats of the same item, and ingredient art. The pantry is in place for it.

## Developer (Settings → Developer mode → Treat toss)
- **Start treat toss now:** opens the mode if needed and starts the toss right after GO.
- **Dials:**
  - Toss length: 5–15 s, default 8.5
  - Treats tossed: 6–30, default 16
  - Treat speed: 0.6–1.6×, default 1.0
  - Tap radius: 26–80, default 44
  
  The dials are saved on the phone, and the section also shows the pantry and a defaults button.
- **Performance log:** carries `tossTuning` and its defaults, the `pantry`, and `tossLog`. `tossLog` holds every flip, catch (with tap distance and treat age), whiff (with the tap point) and drop, with timings, for the last 3 tosses. Events also go to the log's event list.

## Limits
- **Desktop only:** feel, loudness, timing on her phone and FPS need her phone.
- **Difficulty:** the defaults come from scripted players only. The four dials adjust it on the phone.
- **Placeholder:** the fish coin is code-drawn.
- **Sharpness:** treats are the approved sprites drawn at 72 units, so check their sharpness on the phone.

## What to try (sound on)
1. Accept the update. The footer shows **2026.11.12 UAT 45**.
2. Tap **MODES**, then **TREAT TOSS**. Read the card, then tap to start.
3. Play until the fish coin drops (the 3rd–7th bonus drop), then push it off.
4. Catch treats. Try a streak, tap empty air once, and let one fall. You should hear and see the difference each time.
5. Look at the end card: "+N TREATS!", what went to the goal and what went to the pantry.
6. Win a round. The cat dances and a visitor comes.
7. Check that **WILD 2** is just as before: Dance break, music note, no fish coin.
8. Quick try: Settings → Developer mode → Treat toss → **Start treat toss now**. Adjust the four dials if it's too easy or too hard.
