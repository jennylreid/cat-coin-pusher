CP-58 Yawn prototype

The real game (UAT 74) with the new Yawn in place of the old one. Not a release. Tap the board once to turn sound on.

The board needs a moment after a new round starts (countdown). The round clock is topped up each time you play the Yawn.

The beats (about 4.75 s)

  1. 0–1.4 s: big yawn: head back, eyes squeezed, mouth wide, “YAAAWN…”, both mitts stretch up and out and shiver.
  2. 1.5 s: eyes pop open and side-eye up and away.
  3. 1.4–2.25 s: one mitt drapes out and down onto the board, casually.
  4. 2.45 s: it grabs the prize nearest the front (a goal prize first); 2.8 s: the other mitt grabs a second one.
  5. 3.1–4.2 s: both carried to the mouth, four chomps with crumbs, “NOM.”, Happy eyes.
  6. 4.3 s: stolen goal prizes come back as the game's bonus drop: “IT'LL DROP BACK!”.
