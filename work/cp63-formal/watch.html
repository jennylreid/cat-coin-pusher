CP-63 Formal inspection prototype

The real game (UAT 81) with the Tuxedo's power button playing Formal inspection instead of the Yawn sweep. Not a release. Tap the board once to turn the sound on.

Add on top of the pile:

After a new round there is a countdown first. Playing the power adds one charge and tops up the round clock.

The beats (about 4.4 s)

  1. 0–0.45 s: a monocle drops onto the cat's eye with a clink; a glint crosses it.
  2. 0.55–1.95 s: “HMM.” A beam from the monocle and a lens move from piece to piece, back to front; each one gets a gold ring with a ding. The pupils follow the lens.
  3. 1.95–2.45 s: the right mitt brings a cane down behind the lit pieces (whoosh).
  4. 2.45–3.55 s: the cane sweeps forward (swipe), “SPLENDID.”; the lit pieces ride in front of it to the front edge and over it.
  5. 3.9–4.4 s: the mitt goes back, the monocle pops off.
