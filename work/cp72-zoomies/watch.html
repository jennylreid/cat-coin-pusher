CP-72 Zoomies prototype

The real game (UAT 89) on the Orange cat's board, with the power button playing the new Zoomies. Not a release. Tap the board once to turn the sound on.

Add on top of the pile:

After a new round there's a countdown first. Playing the power adds one charge and tops up the round clock.

The beats (revision 2, about 8 s)

  1. 0 s: "ZOOMIES!", the turbo rev and a meow. The pusher runs at double speed for 7 s (turbo slide sound, gold streaks, "2× PUSHER").
  2. 0.4 s and 0.95 s: two kittens pop out at the back (chirp), crouch in a play-bow (front low, rear up, tail hooked, wide eyes), wiggle, and bolt.
  3. They tear down the board in a low sprint, zigzagging, with stunts on the way: a circle, a sideways arched-back hop, a straight-up spook jump (fur and tail puffed straight out), a flip.
  4. What they run into while heading toward you is shoved forward over the front edge: at most 1 prize per use, plus Rain, Quake and top coins (up to 3 per kitten, 6 in all). Other prizes are held at the lip while it runs.
  5. Each kitten skids at the front edge and leaps off toward you, stretched out.
